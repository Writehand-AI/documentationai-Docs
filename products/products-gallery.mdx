The Products area is your home base on Productised. Every AI product you've built lives here — from early-stage drafts to products actively running in the world. It's also the starting point for creating new ones.

## Creating a product

Use the **Create Product** button in the top right to start a new AI product. You'll be taken through the creation flow — choosing a starting point, naming your product, and landing in the canvas where you can begin building.

See [Create your first product](/getting-started/create-with-ai) for a full walkthrough of the creation flow.

## The filter bar

Above your product grid is a filter and sort bar that lets you work with your products efficiently as your library grows.

### Sort

Choose how your products are ordered:

- **Newest first** — most recently created at the top (default)
- **Oldest first** — chronological order
- **Name A → Z** — alphabetical
- **Name Z → A** — reverse alphabetical

### Visibility filter

Filter by how a product is deployed:

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every product regardless of status |
| **Marketplace** | Products listed on the public Marketplace |
| **Public** | Products deployed and accessible via link |
| **Private** | Products not yet deployed |

### Colour filter

If you've assigned colours to your products, colour swatches appear as quick filters. Click a colour to see only products with that colour. Click again to clear. There are eight colours available: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Teal, Blue, Purple, and Pink.

### Legend

The legend in the filter bar also acts as a quick-filter shortcut:

- **Yellow dot** — Marketplace (publicly listed template)
- **Blue dot** — Public (live and deployed)
- **Grey dot** — Private (not yet deployed or unlisted)

Click any legend item to filter instantly.

### Search

The **Search** button opens a quick-search panel to find a specific product by name.

### View mode

Toggle between **Compact** (tighter grid, more products visible) and **Visual** (larger cards with more visual weight). Your preference is saved between sessions.

## Product cards

Each card represents a single AI product. At a glance, the card shows the product's name, its colour label, and its visibility status.

**Card border colours reflect deployment state:**
- **Yellow border** — listed on the Marketplace (or a base template)
- **Blue border** — deployed publicly
- **Grey border** — private or draft

**Clicking a card** opens it in the canvas.

### Card actions

Each card has four action buttons along the bottom:

| Icon | Action |
|---|---|
| **Eye** | Preview the product as a user would see it |
| **Pencil** | Open in the canvas to edit |
| **Link** | Copy the product's shareable link to clipboard |
| **Trash** | Delete the product |

The **Copy link** button copies the product's live URL — the link you'd share with your audience, embed in a campaign, or send to a client.

<Callout kind="tip">The product count shown in the top right of the filter bar ("X of Y products") updates dynamically as you filter — useful for understanding how your library breaks down by visibility or colour.</Callout>

## What happens on the free plan

On the free plan, you can create and edit products as drafts but cannot deploy them publicly. The gallery will show a nudge to upgrade when you're ready to take your products live.

If you're not on a plan at all, the gallery will show an empty state prompting you to upgrade to create products.
