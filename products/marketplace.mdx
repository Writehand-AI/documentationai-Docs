Once you've built a product that works well, you can share it. Productised gives you two ways to do that: a private share link that lets anyone install an exact copy into their own workspace, and the public Marketplace where your product becomes a template available to the entire Productised community.

These are different things with different purposes. Understanding the distinction matters.

## Private share links

Every deployed product has a shareable link. Copy it from the product card in the [Products gallery](/products/products-gallery) using the link icon.

When someone opens that link, they can install an exact copy of your product into their own Productised workspace. They get your structure — your workflow, your nodes, your configuration — and can edit it from there. Nothing is shared between workspaces after the copy is made: their copy is theirs, your original is yours.

**This is the fastest way to share a product directly with someone** — a client, a collaborator, a peer in your network. No public listing required. Just send the link.

Private sharing works for any deployed product, regardless of whether it's listed on the Marketplace.

## Listing on the Marketplace

The Marketplace is the public template library for Productised. When you list a product there, other users can discover it, preview it, and install their own copy.

To list a product on the Marketplace:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy your product">
    Your product must be deployed and set to **Public** visibility. A private or draft product cannot be listed. Go to **Product Settings → Deployment** and ensure your product is live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable List on Marketplace">
    In **Product Settings → Deployment**, scroll to the **List on Marketplace** toggle and enable it. This makes your product discoverable in the public Marketplace and marks its card with a yellow border in your gallery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">Complete your product thoroughly before listing it — a clear name, a descriptive setup, and a well-configured workflow make your template genuinely useful to others. Templates that deliver clear, well-structured starting points are the ones that get used.</Callout>

Once listed, your product appears in the Marketplace with its name and configuration. Other users can preview the product's structure and install a copy into their workspace with one click. They cannot modify your original — they only ever receive a copy.

To remove a product from the Marketplace, disable the **List on Marketplace** toggle in Deployment Settings. It will be delisted immediately. Your product remains deployed and accessible via its direct link — only the Marketplace listing is removed.

## Private link vs Marketplace — when to use each

| | Private link | Marketplace listing |
|---|---|---|
| **Who can access it** | Anyone with the link | All Productised users |
| **Discovery** | Only if you share the link directly | Publicly discoverable |
| **Best for** | Sharing with specific people | Making a template broadly available |
| **Requires deployment** | Yes | Yes |
| **Requires Marketplace toggle** | No | Yes |

## Sharing and installing via Claude

If you've connected the [Productised Claude connector](/claude-connector/overview), you can do both flows directly from a Claude conversation.

* **Get a share link for your own product** — *"Give me a share link for my Pricing Health Check product"* → Claude calls `get_template_share_link` and hands you the marketplace install URL. Anyone you forward it to lands on the marketplace install page and clicks Copy to Workspace.
* **Browse the public marketplace** — *"Show me marketplace templates for coaches"* or *"What lead-generation templates exist?"* → Claude calls `list_marketplace_templates` and renders a visual gallery. Each card has Install (opens the marketplace install URL) and Preview (opens the live demo). Filters: industry, GROW stage (G/R/O/W), keyword search.

Install always happens on the web — the marketplace install page handles the plan-limit check and creates the workspace copy. Claude only hands you the URL.

## The Marketplace as a distribution channel

For consultants and educators who build high-quality products, the Marketplace is a passive distribution channel. A well-built lead generation template, a client onboarding product, or a diagnostic tool listed publicly becomes a way for other practitioners to discover your work — and, through it, you.

Every product listed under your workspace carries your brand. Build templates you're proud of.

<Callout kind="info">The Marketplace is an evolving part of Productised. Templates, categories, and discovery features are actively being developed. Products you list now will be part of the library as it grows.</Callout>
