Productised is a platform for building **AI products** — personalised, on-brand experiences your audience goes through to receive something genuinely useful. A diagnostic that maps their specific situation. A scorecard that grades them across the dimensions that matter. A consultation-style advisor that helps them think. A custom branded results page that feels like it was made for one person — because it was.

You build the product once. Your audience goes through it any time. They receive an outcome that's specific to them. You receive a qualified lead with everything you need to follow up well.

## The problem Productised solves

For decades, the way you collected information from a prospect, client, or audience was the same: a form, maybe a quiz, maybe a redirect to a results page that said "thanks for submitting." The prospect handed you their name, their email, their situation — their time — and what they got back was something that didn't know who they were.

Quiz funnels and conditional logic tried to evolve this. Branching flows, score-based outcomes, persona-based results. For a while that was better. But conditional logic has a ceiling — the complexity required to create *truly* personalised outcomes grows exponentially with every dimension you add. Setup takes weeks. Every new use case means rebuilding the logic tree from scratch.

And then AI arrived. Most platforms bolted it on as a feature. The result: tools that look smarter than they are, and audiences who are getting increasingly good at spotting the difference.

**Productised is built from the ground up around a different idea.** The AI isn't an add-on — it's the engine. You design how the conversation should go, what your expertise should weigh, and what the deliverable should look like. The platform produces a unique, on-brand outcome for every person who goes through it.

## What an AI product looks like

An AI product is a single, shareable URL. A respondent opens it, has a short conversation with an AI you've configured, and receives a personalised outcome. That's it.

Behind the URL, you've designed:

- **The conversation** — what the AI asks, in what order, in what tone
- **The intelligence** — what the AI weighs, what it scores, what it concludes
- **The outcome** — a streamed report, a live AI advisor session, or a custom HTML results page
- **The brand** — colours, fonts, logo, custom domain, SEO

Every respondent gets something different — because the AI is generating it for them in real time. Two people fill out the same product and receive two different outcomes, framed around what they actually said.

## Brain — your strategic AI assistant

Every Productised workspace includes **Brain** — a strategic AI assistant that helps you decide *what* to build, *who* it's for, and *why* it will work in your market.

Brain isn't a chatbot. It knows the platform inside out — it only suggests products you can actually build, grounded in your niche and your existing suite. Ask it what to build next, give it context about your audience, and it surfaces a ready-to-build concept. One click opens it onto your canvas, or you can tell Claude to build it directly through the connector.

Brain also builds long-term memory of your workspace — your positioning, your audience, your methodology — so every session starts where the last one left off. The **Journey Plan** turns that into a full-funnel roadmap across all five stages of your client journey, grounded in your specific niche.
