You've spent years building expertise. You know what questions to ask, what patterns to spot, and what advice changes things for people. Productised turns that knowledge into something your clients can experience — not just read about.

Productised is a platform for coaches and consultants to build AI-powered products: lead generation tools, scorecards, diagnostic assessments, strategy generators, personalised offers, and more. No code. No developers. No waiting.

## What kind of products can you build?

Think of the conversations you have with new clients. The questions you always ask. The way you quickly understand where someone is stuck and what they need. Productised lets you package that process into something that runs automatically — at any scale, at any hour.

Some examples of what people build on Productised:

- A **business readiness assessment** that asks clients ten questions and delivers a personalised diagnostic report
- A **lead qualification tool** that identifies ideal clients and presents them with a tailored offer
- A **strategy generator** that collects context through conversation and produces a bespoke action plan they can download
- A **scorecard** that evaluates someone's current situation and serves up a personalised next step
- A **discovery call replacement** that gathers everything you need to know before the client ever books with you

Every product you build on Productised is a conversational AI experience — not a static form, not a generic chatbot, but something that feels like a real one-to-one interaction with an expert.

## Who is Productised for?

Productised is built for professionals who sell expertise: coaches, consultants, strategists, advisors, and specialists of every kind. You don't need to know how to code, configure APIs, or think about infrastructure. If you understand your audience and know what questions to ask, you have everything you need.

## How it all fits together

Every product on Productised is built on a simple idea: collect the right information from the right people, use AI to make sense of it, and deliver something genuinely personalised and valuable. The platform handles the AI, the delivery, and the data — you focus on the expertise that makes the outcome worth having.
