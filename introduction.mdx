AI is no longer a differentiator on its own. Every platform is adding AI features. Every competitor is automating something. The question for coaches and consultants isn't whether to use AI — it's whether the AI you use makes you look exceptional, or just adequate.

Productised exists for professionals who refuse to settle for adequate.

## What an AI product actually is

An AI product is not a chatbot. It is not a form with a smart summary at the end. It is not an agent that does tasks on your behalf.

An AI product is a client-facing experience — something a prospect or client goes through once, or a few times, that draws on your expertise, collects what it needs to know about them, and delivers something tangible: a personalised report, a strategy document, a commercial recommendation, a next step that feels like it was made for them.

In plain terms, here is what an AI product looks like in practice:

- A **lead diagnostic** that asks six questions about someone's business, evaluates their situation against your framework, and delivers a personalised insight report — with a CTA that routes high-fit leads straight to your calendar
- A **readiness scorecard** that walks a prospect through a structured conversation, scores their responses, and delivers a visual breakdown of where they stand — ending with an offer that fits what they actually need
- A **strategy brief generator** that collects context from a new client, runs it through your methodology, and produces a formatted 90-day plan they can download before your first call
- A **market snapshot tool** that takes someone's business type and target audience, researches live market conditions, and generates a personalised industry brief — a lead magnet that actually earns attention
- A **discovery call replacement** that gathers everything you need to know before anyone books time with you — so when they do, the conversation starts at a completely different level

These are not utilities. They are not tools clients use every day. They are high-value, one-time (or few-time) experiences that do the work of a good first conversation — at any scale, at any hour, for anyone who finds you.

## Why Productised is different

Anyone can build an AI tool now. Anyone can deploy an AI agent. The technology is accessible, the models are capable, and the barrier to entry is lower every month.

What nobody else gives you is this combination: a beautiful branded experience that feels like you, AI reasoning that acts like your expert alter ego, and tangible outcomes designed to convert strangers into clients.

Most AI platforms deliver platform artifacts — results that live inside the tool, visible only in a dashboard, inaccessible once the session ends. Productised delivers outcomes that exist in the world. Reports that get saved. Documents that get downloaded. Offers that get acted on.

And while most platforms are building AI tools, Productised was built for AI products — designed from the start around what coaches and consultants actually deliver: expertise that moves people, positioned and packaged so it scales without losing the personal touch.

The canvas is built on connected nodes by design. You see exactly what your AI product draws on. You add what you need — a Research Power to pull in live market data, a Knowledge Power to ground responses in your framework, a Visual Insights Power to make scores land harder — and nothing more. Powers are modular because transparency and control matter when your reputation is attached to the output.

## Who Productised is built for

If you sell expertise — as a coach, consultant, strategist, advisor, or specialist — Productised is built for you.

You do not need to know how to code. You do not need to configure APIs or manage infrastructure. If you understand your audience and know what questions to ask, you have everything you need to build your first AI product.

You are the go-to expert in your field. Productised is the engine that makes that expertise scalable — without turning it into something generic.
