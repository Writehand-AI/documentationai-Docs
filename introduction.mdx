## Overview

Productised empowers you to build AI-powered assessments with no code required. You create interactive experiences that capture leads while instantly generating personalized, branded outcome pages and reports. This approach transforms traditional quizzes and forms into dynamic tools that deliver high perceived value and boost conversions.

## Quick Start

Get your first assessment live in under 10 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit [app.productised.ai](https://app.productised.ai) and create your free account. No credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Assessment" icon="plus">
    Click "New Assessment" and choose a template or start from scratch. Define your questions using the intuitive builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize AI Outputs" icon="zap">
    Set up your branding and configure AI prompts for personalized reports. Preview outcomes in real-time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish & Embed" icon="globe">
    Generate embed code or share your assessment link. Track leads in your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Embed on Website" icon="code">
    Copy the embed snippet:

    ```
    <script src="https://app.productised.ai/embed.js" data-id="your-assessment-id"></script>
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Share Link" icon="link">
    Use your unique assessment URL: `https://app.productised.ai/assessment/your-id`
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
