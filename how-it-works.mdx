Every product you build on Productised follows the same simple loop. You build the experience once. Your audience goes through it at any time. They receive something personalised and valuable on the other end. That's it.

<Callout kind="info">🎥 **Video:** A 60-second loop showing the full journey — a coach building a product on the canvas, connecting a Power and an Outcome, then switching to a preview of the end-user experience going through the AI conversation, and finally the personalised report appearing on screen.</Callout>

## The three parts of every product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build your product on the canvas">
    The canvas is your workspace — a visual, drag-and-drop environment where you design how your product works. You start with your **AI Product**, which is the conversational experience your audience goes through. Then you add **Powers** to give the AI additional capabilities (like pulling in knowledge from your documents or running a scoring evaluation), and connect an **Outcome** to define what your audience receives at the end.

    You don't write code on the canvas. You design a flow, like a blueprint, and Productised handles everything that happens behind the scenes. You can also chain multiple AI nodes together in sequence — each one doing a specific job before passing its output to the next.

    <Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The canvas with an AI Product node in the centre, a Knowledge Power connected on the left, a Scoring node on the right, and a Report Outcome connected at the bottom</Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Your audience goes through it">
    When your product is live, anyone with the link — or an embed on your website — can access it. They're taken through a personalised AI conversation that feels like talking to an expert. The AI asks one question at a time, adapts based on what they share, and gathers everything it needs to deliver a meaningful outcome.

    Your audience could be a lead discovering you for the first time, an existing client going through a check-in process, or anyone you want to give a premium experience to.

    <Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The end-user AI conversation interface — showing a conversational question on a dark, branded background with a response input at the bottom</Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="They receive a personalised outcome">
    Once the conversation is complete, the AI generates the outcome you configured — a diagnostic report, a personalised document, or a redirect to the right next step. Each one is different because each conversation is different. The AI uses everything the person shared to make it specific to them.

    You see every response in your analytics dashboard, with each person's answers and the outcome they received.

    <Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** An example AI-generated report outcome page with personalised sections, insights, and a CTA button</Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where Brain fits in

**Brain** is separate from the products you build — it's your strategic AI assistant for deciding *what* to build.

Use Brain when you're thinking about your product strategy, exploring new funnel gaps, or you want a confident recommendation for what to build next. Brain knows your workspace — your existing products, your niche, your positioning — and surfaces build-ready concepts you can open on the canvas with a single click.
