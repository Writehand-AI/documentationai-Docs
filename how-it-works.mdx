## The three stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build — design the experience" title-type="p">
    You design how your product works once. There are no recurring "campaigns" to manage and no logic trees to maintain.

    A product has three layers:

    - **An AI Product node** — the conversational engine. You define the questions to collect, the tone of the conversation, and the brand colours / fonts of the interface.
    - **Optional Powers** — capabilities you attach to extend the AI: Knowledge (your documents), Research (live web search), Scoring (numeric evaluation), and Connections (downstream integrations).
    - **An Outcome** — what your audience receives: a streamed standard output, a live AI advisor session, a custom HTML results page, or a routed redirect.

    You can build this in the **canvas** (a visual drag-and-drop workspace) or via the [**Claude Connector**](/claude-connector/overview) — both edit the same workspace.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781832033371-i1omrjr9wz-Canvas1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2926" height="1828" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run — your audience goes through it" title-type="p">
    When the product is live, anyone with the link can access it. They're taken through an AI conversation that feels like talking to an expert. The AI asks one question at a time, adapts based on what they share, and gathers everything it needs to deliver a meaningful outcome.

    Your audience could be a lead discovering you for the first time, an existing client going through a check-in, or anyone you want to give a premium experience to. The conversation is personalised to _them_, in your voice, against your framework.

    No two conversations are the same — because the AI is responding to what each person actually says.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781832237433-d9ei4qspz1s-Outcome-Example.Outcome Example?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1541" height="2500" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deliver — they receive a personalised outcome" title-type="p">
    Once the conversation is complete, the AI generates the outcome you configured. Depending on the type:

    - A **streamed report** writes itself into the chat — a personalised analysis, document, or strategy.
    - A **custom result page** (HTML) renders at a public URL, filled with their scores, insights, and a personalised CTA.
    - A **live advisor** continues the conversation with full context — they can keep asking questions.
    - A **routed redirect** sends them to the right next step — your calendar, a nurture sequence, a checkout.

    Every outcome is unique. You see every response in your analytics dashboard, with each person's answers and the outcome they received.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781832468705-z2l2ksb6tt-Product-Analytics.Product Analytics?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2940" height="4482" alt="" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The two surfaces

Productised is one platform with two ways to drive it. Both edit the same workspace in real time — pick whichever feels natural in the moment, or use both in the same session.

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="The Productised Canvas " href="/canvas/understanding-the-canvas" icon="layout" horizontal="false" target="_self">
    A visual workspace where you build products by dragging nodes onto a graph and connecting them. Best for first-time exploration, complex multi-node flows, and visual thinkers. No code required.
  </Card>

  <Card title="The Claude Connector" href="/claude-connector/overview" icon="sparkles" horizontal="false">
    Drive everything from a Claude conversation — 73 live tools against your workspace. Best for fast iteration, designing custom result pages conversationally, and people who already live in Claude.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Most users start with our Claude connector. Power users are jumping into the Productised app to build the structure on the canvas (where the visual layout helps you reason about connections), then iterate copy, system prompts, and general settings.

## Where Brain fits in

[Brain](/brain/what-is-brain) is a separate layer from the products you build. Where products are built _for_ your audience, Brain is built _for you_ — your strategic AI assistant for deciding what to build next.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781832755224-7mh7ws4tibl-The-Brain.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2928" height="1832" alt="" />

Use Brain when you're thinking about product strategy, exploring funnel gaps, or you want a confident recommendation for what to build first. Brain knows your workspace — your existing products, your niche, your positioning, your previous ideas — and surfaces build-ready concepts you can open on the canvas or hand to Claude.

The **Journey Plan** turns Brain's knowledge into a full-funnel product roadmap across all five stages of your client journey. It tells you what to build, in what order, and why — grounded in your specific niche. \
\
However, it is only available to users once they have enough data in their account.\
\
Ensure to complete your onboarding brand voice settings first!
