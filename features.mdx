# The Brain

The Brain is your workspace-level strategic AI assistant. Unlike AI Products — which are built for your audience — Brain is built for you. It helps you decide what to build, how to position it, and where it fits in your funnel.

Use Brain when you're thinking about product strategy, exploring funnel gaps, or you want a confident recommendation for what to build first. Brain knows your workspace — your existing products, your niche, your positioning, your previous ideas — and surfaces build-ready concepts you can open on the canvas or hand to Claude.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The brain is a **powerful** feature. In order to get the best value from the brain, ensure to enter as much information about your business and offerings in the onboarding or master settings > brand voice. It is designed specifically to aid users in building products that deliver value to their particular audience, rather than provide generic results pages that decrease chances of prospects taking the next step.&#x20;
</Callout>

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1791758118550-4dvykc2nacf-screencapture-app-productised-ai-app-productised-brain-2026-10-12-11_34_55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2926" height="1462" alt="Productised Brain" />

Brain has five unique sections:

- **Chat** — have a strategy conversation. Ask it to help you design a product, stress-test an idea, or figure out where your funnel is weakest.
- **Memory** — Brain automatically saves context from every conversation — your niche, audience, methodology, and preferences. Every session starts from where the last one left off.
- **Sources** — Brain has its own knowledge base. Upload your methodology documents, frameworks, and positioning material. Brain draws on them in every conversation.
- **Ideas** — The brain produces build-ready concepts you've discussed. You can open any product idea directly on the canvas to start building immediately.
- **Journey Plan** — generate a full-funnel product roadmap across all five stages of your client journey (ToFU, MoFU, BoFU, Delivery, Community). Brain synthesises everything it knows about your practice into a prioritised, niche-specific plan — showing what to build, in what order, and why.

# Product Editing: The Canvas

The canvas is your building workspace. Every product you create lives here as a visual graph — drag nodes onto the canvas, connect them with edges, and wire up your logic without writing a line of code. Changes save automatically as you work.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Node types fall into three categories: **AI Product** (the conversational core), **capabilities** you attach to it (typically AI agents), and **Outcomes** (the deliverables your audience receives). You can have multiple nodes of each type on a single canvas and can also chain them together for complex products.
</Callout>

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1791768343602-8t6pwt9eke6-screencapture-app-productised-ai-products-cmuuiq9zt000p356oh8ns2tum-canvas-2026-10-12-14_25_20.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1686" height="934" alt="Productised Canvas" />

## AI Product Node

The AI Product node is the conversational engine inside every product. It collects inputs from your audience through a structured flow — text fields, choice questions, scales, yes/no prompts, image selections, and more — then processes those inputs through your configured AI model to produce a personalised result.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Each AI Product has a **collection goal** (what you're trying to learn), a **decision model** (how the AI evaluates responses — AI Judgement or AI Scorecard), and optional scoring to segment your audience into persona profiles.&#x20;
</Callout>

### Prompt Chaining

You're not limited to a single AI Agent node. Add multiple AI Agent nodes to the canvas and connect them in sequence — the output of one becomes the input of the next. Reference outputs using `{{ node:varName }}` syntax anywhere downstream.

This lets you build sophisticated multi-step products: collect information in the first node, run a scoring pass in the second, generate a detailed report in the third. Each step can have its own prompt, model, and configuration.

## MCP Node (Advanced)

Your AI Product runs the conversation and writes the result on its own. The MCP node is how you extend it beyond that.

**MCP connections** — connect any service that publishes an MCP server: your CRM, your calendar, a search provider, your own internal API. Claude can call that service's tools _while an end user is answering_, so it can check a record, confirm live availability or look something up before deciding what to recommend.

Because MCP is an open standard, this isn't a fixed list of integrations — you connect the providers you already use, with your own account and credentials.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1791768516037-d2qpvwyku5q-screencapture-app-productised-ai-products-cmuuiq9zt000p356oh8ns2tum-canvas-2026-10-12-14_28_30.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1686" height="985" alt="Productised MCP Node" />

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Only tools the server declares read-only are enabled when you first connect. Anything that could create, edit or delete data waits until you switch it on, because the AI calls these tools unsupervised while talking to a member of the public.
</Callout>

## Deployment

When your product is ready, deploy it in seconds. Options include:

- **Share link** — a hosted URL you can send directly or embed in a button
- **Website embed** — drop a snippet into any website or landing page
- **Custom domain** — map your own domain to the product
- **Product settings** — control visibility, collect email, set redirect behaviour after completion

* **Webhooks** — post each completed response to any URL, so leads and AI output reach your CRM, email platform or automation tool.
