## What Productised is <u>not</u>

Understanding what Productised doesn't do is just as important as understanding what it does.





<SVG src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1785640198973-9z31kyduvtb-SittingDoodle.svg?rasterize-bypass=true" />



Productised is **not a vibe coding/app building tool**. Platforms built for developers — where you describe what you want and the AI writes the code for it — are powerful, but they're designed for people who are comfortable reviewing code, deploying servers, and debugging logic. They can build almost anything, which also means they're responsible for almost everything.

Productised makes a deliberate choice to do one thing exceptionally well: client-facing AI-powered products for professionals who sell expertise. The canvas, the Powers, the Outcomes — all of it is designed specifically for lead generation and client delivery. Think AI-Powered quizzes, scorecards, assessments, deliverables etc. There is no code to write, no infrastructure to manage, and no open-ended complexity to navigate.

That constraint is intentional. **Simplicity in the right direction is a feature, not a limitation. We did this as many non-coders who were 'vibe coding' rarely shared their creations with actual clients.&#x20;**

Similarly, Productised is not a general-purpose AI workflow builder. Workflow builders are designed for internal automation — recurring processes, system integrations, background tasks. They require technical thinking and are built for teams who use tools repeatedly day after day.

Your clients and prospects are not going to use your product every day. They're going to go through it once — or a handful of times — and receive something that makes them think you are exactly who they need to work with. **_That's the use case Productised is optimised for._**&#x20;

If you're comparing Productised to platforms where the "outcome" is a results screen inside the tool itself — a score, a label, a short summary that lives in a dashboard — you're comparing it to the wrong thing. Productised delivers outcomes that exist in the world: reports saved, documents downloaded, offers acted on. These are the moments that convert, and adapt and get better as AI models get better as well.&#x20;

## How to create your first product

The fastest way to get started is to let the AI build your product for you. You describe what you want to create, and the AI generates a fully configured product — conversation structure, questions, decision logic, and outcome — ready for you to review, refine, and publish.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781837557175-34kzuan8s1m-Productised-Dashboard.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2936" height="1836" alt="" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your product and describe what it should do" title-type="p">
    Give your product a name and describe your idea in plain English. Think of this as briefing a colleague — the more specific you are, the better the result.

    Good descriptions include:

    - Who your audience is ("coaches who are thinking about launching an online course")
    - What the product should help them understand or decide ("whether they're ready to scale their business")
    - What kind of outcome you want to deliver ("a personalised readiness score with a recommendation")

    You do not need to use technical language. Write the way you'd explain it to a friend.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781837611792-65s00wouxm4-Create-Product.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2918" height="1712" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="The AI analyses your brief and builds the product" title-type="p">
    Once you submit your description, the AI analyses your intent, identifies the right product type, designs the conversation flow, creates the collection questions, and configures the decision logic that determines what outcome each person receives. It may also ask further qualifying questions to ensure it builds the product correctly and with a purpose.&#x20;

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781837768683-u7znz0ugj6-Productised2.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2924" height="1828" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review what the AI created" title-type="p">
    When generation is complete, you'll see a summary of what the AI built: the product type it chose, the questions it created, your target audience as it understood it, the collection style it selected, and what happens at the end of the conversation.

    This is your chance to check whether the AI understood your intent. If something looks off, you can adjust the product intent. However, everything is fully editable on the canvas.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781837816252-epd5y3bj0zt-Product3.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2932" height="1830" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the canvas and make it yours" title-type="p">
    From the summary screen, open your canvas. You'll see your AI Product node fully configured. You can:

    - Edit the welcome message your audience sees first
    - Adjust or reorder the questions the AI asks
    - Change the outcome type (Report, Document, Offer)
    - Add Powers to extend what the AI can do
    - Customise colours, fonts, and branding in the Design tab

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781838609526-586zvmyv149-Canvas2.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2930" height="1832" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and publish" title-type="p">
    Before sharing, use the preview link on the AI Product node to go through the experience as your audience would. Check that the conversation feels natural and the outcome is what you expected.

    When you're ready, copy your product link from the Deployment settings and share it — or embed it on your website.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781838789597-9dkmjq96d07-Canvasa.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2928" height="1830" alt="" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything the AI creates is a starting point, not a final draft. You can edit and customise every part of what it builds — questions, prompts, outcome settings, and design.
