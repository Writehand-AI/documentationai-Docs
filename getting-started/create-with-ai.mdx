The fastest way to get started on Productised is to let the AI build your product for you. You describe what you want to create, and the AI generates a fully configured product — conversation structure, questions, decision logic, and outcome — ready for you to review, refine, and publish.

This is called **Create with AI**, and it's the recommended starting point for anyone new to the platform.

<Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The "Create with AI" entry point — the new product modal or button that triggers the quick-generate flow</Callout>

## How to create your first product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your product and describe what it should do">
    When you create a new product, you'll be asked to give it a name and describe your idea in plain English. Think of this as briefing a colleague — the more specific you are, the better the result.

    Good descriptions include:
    - Who your audience is ("coaches who are thinking about launching an online course")
    - What the product should help them understand or decide ("whether they're ready to scale their business")
    - What kind of outcome you want to deliver ("a personalised readiness score with a recommendation")

    You don't need to use technical language. Write the way you'd explain it to a friend.

    <Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The product name and description input screen with an example filled in — e.g. "Business Readiness Assessment" with a description like "Help coaches see whether they're ready to take their business to the next level, then deliver a personalised recommendation."</Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="The AI analyses your brief and builds the product">
    Once you submit your description, the AI gets to work. It analyses your intent, identifies the right product type and template, designs the conversation flow, creates the collection questions (the things it will ask your audience), and configures the decision logic that determines what outcome each person receives.

    You'll see a progress screen as this happens — it typically takes 20–40 seconds.

    <Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The AI generation progress screen showing status steps completing — "Analysing your brief", "Building conversation flow", "Configuring outcome"</Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review what the AI created">
    When generation is complete, you'll see a summary of what the AI built: the product type it chose, how many questions it created, your target audience as it understood it, and what happens at the end of the conversation.

    This is your chance to check whether the AI understood your intent. If something looks off, you can adjust it on the canvas — everything the AI creates is fully editable.

    <Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The post-generation summary screen showing the product name, template chosen, number of collection questions, and an overview of the outcome type</Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the canvas and make it yours">
    From the summary screen, click through to your canvas. You'll see your AI Product node in the centre with all the settings already configured. You can:

    - Edit the welcome message your audience sees first
    - Adjust or reorder the questions the AI will ask
    - Change the outcome type (Report, Document, Offer)
    - Add Powers to give the AI extra capabilities
    - Customise the colours, fonts, and branding

    <Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The canvas view immediately after AI generation — showing the AI Product node with settings panel open on the right, highlighting the Content tab</Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and publish">
    Before sharing your product, use the preview link on the AI Product node to go through the experience as your audience would. Check that the conversation feels natural, the questions make sense, and the outcome is what you expected.

    When you're happy, copy your product link from the product settings and share it — or embed it on your website.

    <Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The AI Product node on the canvas with the preview link visible, and a small browser window showing the live chat experience loading</Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">Everything the AI creates is a starting point, not a final draft. You can always edit and customise every part of what it builds — the questions, the prompts, the outcome settings, and the design.</Callout>

## What comes next

Now that your product is created, the most important thing to understand is your **AI Product node** — the core of every product on Productised. It controls how your AI converses with your audience, what it collects, and how it decides what to deliver.
