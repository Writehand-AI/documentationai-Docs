## How to create your first product

The fastest way to get started is to let the AI build your product for you. You describe what you want to create, and the AI generates a fully configured product — conversation structure, questions, decision logic, and outcome — ready for you to review, refine, and publish.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your product and describe what it should do" title-type="p">
    Give your product a name and describe your idea in plain English. Think of this as briefing a colleague — the more specific you are, the better the result.

    Good descriptions include:

    - Who your audience is ("coaches who are thinking about launching an online course")
    - What the product should help them understand or decide ("whether they're ready to scale their business")
    - What kind of outcome you want to deliver ("a personalised readiness score with a recommendation")

    You do not need to use technical language. Write the way you'd explain it to a friend.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1791758658337-pjg9gxdn2o-screencapture-app-productised-ai-app-productised-products-quick-create-2026-10-12-11_43_05.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2926" height="1654" alt="screencapture app productised ai app productised products quick create 2026 10 12 11 43 05" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="The AI analyses your brief and builds the product" title-type="p">
    Once you submit your description, the AI analyses your intent, identifies the right product type, designs the conversation flow, creates the collection questions, and configures the decision logic that determines what outcome each person receives. It may also ask further qualifying questions to ensure it builds the product correctly and with a purpose.&#x20;

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1791758724536-kwle6sj5uh-screencapture-app-productised-ai-app-productised-products-quick-create-2026-10-12-11_45_26.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2926" height="1654" alt="screencapture app productised ai app productised products quick create 2026 10 12 11 45 26" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review what the AI created" title-type="p">
    When generation is complete, you'll see a summary of what the AI built: the product type it chose, the questions it created, your target audience as it understood it, the collection style it selected, and what happens at the end of the conversation.

    This is your chance to check whether the AI understood your intent. If something looks off, you can adjust the product intent. However, everything is fully editable on the canvas.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1781837816252-epd5y3bj0zt-Product3.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2932" height="1830" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the canvas and make it yours" title-type="p">
    From the summary screen, open your canvas. You'll see your AI Product node fully configured. You can:

    - Edit the welcome message your audience sees first
    - Adjust or reorder the questions the AI asks
    - Change the outcome type (Collect only, Standard, Page, Advisor)
    - Add Powers to extend what the AI can do
    - Customise colours, fonts, and branding in the Design tab

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1791758299297-o494w98bbo9-Screenshot-2026-10-12-at-11-38-23-AM.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2918" height="1454" alt="Screenshot 2026 10 12 at 11.38.23 AM" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and publish" title-type="p">
    When you're ready, copy your product link from the Deployment settings and share it — or embed it on your website.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-708d97b3-a195-4b0f-8911-093c012c99d6/doc-8283fb6b-099f-47ea-b32b-34e7f582103c/1791758896010-oc08lantas-screencapture-app-productised-ai-products-cmuuiq9zt000p356oh8ns2tum-settings-2026-10-12-11_48_07.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2926" height="5304" alt="screencapture app productised ai products cmuuiq9zt000p356oh8ns2tum settings 2026 10 12 11 48 07" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything the AI creates is a starting point, not a final draft. You can edit and customise every part of what it builds — questions, prompts, outcome settings, and design.
