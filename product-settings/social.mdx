The Social tab generates platform-specific social media posts for your product — copy for LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Facebook — using AI. Each generation produces three variants per platform. Available on Starter plan and above.

<Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The Social tab showing three platform cards (LinkedIn, X, Facebook) each with a generated post, character count, and copy button visible</Callout>

## How it works

Click **Generate Posts**. The AI reads your product name, description, the collection questions in your product, and the workflow nodes to understand what your product does and who it is for. It then generates three variants of a social post for each of the three platforms, calibrated to the tone and character conventions of each.

You get three variants per platform so you can pick the one that fits your voice best, or use them as a starting point for your own copy.

## Platform conventions

**LinkedIn** — Professional tone, under 700 characters for optimal reach (the hard limit is 3,000). Posts are written to perform in a professional context: credibility-forward, audience-specific, outcome-oriented.

**X (Twitter)** — Punchy and direct, under 257 characters (the hard limit is 280, but shorter performs better). The URL to your product is included and counts as approximately 23 characters.

**Facebook** — Conversational, under 500 characters for best engagement. Warmer tone than LinkedIn.

## Using the variants

Each platform card shows the generated post with a character count. Use the arrow buttons to cycle between the three variants. Click **Copy** to copy the post text to your clipboard, then paste it directly into the platform.

You can edit the copied text before posting. The generated copy is a starting point — adjust the voice, the specifics, or the framing to match your own style.

## Generation limit

Social post generation is limited to 3 generations per product per day to prevent abuse of the AI generation quota. The remaining generation count is shown in the tab. Generations reset at midnight.

## Tips for better posts

The quality of the generated social copy depends on the quality of your product configuration. Products with a clear name, a well-written description, and collection goals that have specific, descriptive labels will generate better posts than products with generic field names.

If the generated copy does not feel right, check that your product name and description accurately reflect what the product does and who it is for. These are the primary inputs the AI uses to understand what to write about.
