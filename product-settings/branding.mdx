The Branding tab controls the logo shown on your product's public pages and where users are taken when they click it. Available on Starter plan and above.

<Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The Branding settings tab showing the Custom Branding Redirect URL field, the toggle switched on, and a logo uploaded and visible</Callout>

## Custom Branding Redirect URL

The URL users are taken to when they click the logo on your product page. Set this to your website homepage, a specific landing page, or wherever you want client attention directed.

Example: `https://www.yourcompany.com`

If left blank, the logo is still shown but is not clickable.

## Custom Branding toggle

Enables or disables the custom logo entirely. When off, the default Productised branding appears. When on, your uploaded logo is displayed in the product UI.

Toggle this off if you want to temporarily revert to default branding without deleting your uploaded logo. Toggling back on restores your previous logo.

## Logo upload

When Custom Branding is enabled, a logo upload area appears. Your logo is displayed in the bottom-left corner of your product's public pages.

- Click the upload area or drag and drop an image file
- Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, GIF
- Recommended: upload a transparent-background PNG for the best result across different page backgrounds

To replace an existing logo, click or drag a new file over the upload area.

## Plan availability

Custom branding is available on the Starter plan and above. If you are on the Free plan, the Branding tab will show a notice that an upgrade is required.
