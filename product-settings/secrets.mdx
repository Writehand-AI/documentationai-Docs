Product Settings no longer has its own key tab. Your own Claude API key is set once for your **account** or for a **workspace**, and every product it covers uses it automatically.

## Where to add your key

Open **Settings → AI & connections → Claude API Key** (Admins only). It works on any plan.

- **Account key** — the default for every workspace on the account.
- **This workspace** card — a key for the workspace you're in, which takes priority over the account key. Switch workspace first to set a key for a different one.

To get a key, visit [console.anthropic.com/settings/keys](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys). Keys look like `sk-ant-...`, and are encrypted before they're stored.

## What your key covers

When a key covers a product, its conversations and results run on your Anthropic account and use no Productised credits. That includes the conversation and Advisor chat, AI Agents, scoring, and Standard, Page and Document results.

These still run on Productised's services, at no credit cost:

- Brain and Build with AI
- AI Insights, Talking Points and the Growth Playbook
- Voice input in the chat

Your Anthropic account's spending limits, rate limits and billing rules apply. If your key is rejected or runs out of credit, Productised doesn't switch to its own key — Owners and Admins get an alert, and a replaced key works straight away.

See [Credits](/workspace/credits) for how credits work without your own key, and [Billing a client's AI to them](/agency/client-api-keys) for workspace keys on the Agency plan.
