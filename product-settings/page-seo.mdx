The Page SEO tab controls how your product's public page appears in two places: **search results** (Google, browser tab) and **link previews** (the card platforms show when your link is shared). Available on Starter plan and above.

<Callout kind="tip">
**You're covered by default.** Every published product automatically gets a branded 1200 × 630 share card — generated on the fly with your product's title and description. Share your link on LinkedIn with zero setup and it unfurls with a proper card. Everything below is optional customisation.
</Callout>

## The three sections

The tab is organised by outcome, not by protocol:

**1. Share card** — how your link appears when shared on **LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp and Slack**. These platforms all read the same underlying tags (Open Graph), so you configure the card once and it works everywhere. Fields: share title, share description, and image.

**2. Search result** — how the page appears in Google and the browser tab: page title (50–60 characters) and meta description (120–160 characters). Rarely-used fields (keywords, author, canonical URL, favicon upload) live under **Advanced**.

**3. X (Twitter)** — X reads its own tag set but falls back to your Share card automatically. Only expand this section if you want X-specific overrides (card type, title, description, image).

## The share image: auto-generated or custom

Under **Share card → Image** you choose:

- **Auto-generated card (recommended)** — Productised renders a branded 1200 × 630 card with your share title and description. It updates automatically whenever you change the text, and it personalises per recipient (see below). This is the default — an empty image field means "use the generated card".
- **Custom image** — upload your own (JPG, PNG, WEBP or GIF — 1200 × 630px recommended) or paste a URL. Uploads are saved immediately.

## Personalised share previews

Share title and share description support **personalisation tokens** — the same `{{ url:… }}` tokens used by [outbound personalisation](/outbound-personalisation/overview):

```
{{ url:firstName | there }}, your Pricing Health Check
```

The part after `url:` is the **parameter name**; the part after `|` is the fallback. When someone opens (or a platform previews) a link like `…/c/your-product?firstName=Sarah`, the card reads **"Sarah, your Pricing Health Check"** — in both the text and the generated card image. A bare link shows the fallback ("there, your Pricing Health Check").

Use the **"+ First name"** and **"+ Company"** chips under each field to insert correctly-formed tokens, and the **"Test a personalised link"** box in the preview panel to type a sample name, watch the card update live, and copy a ready-made personalised link.

<Callout kind="info">
Combined with the [CSV outbound link generator](/outbound-personalisation/overview), every prospect in your list gets a share link whose LinkedIn/Slack preview greets them by name — no per-link setup.
</Callout>

## Platform previews

The preview panel shows your card rendered in each platform's real frame — switch between **LinkedIn**, **Google**, **Facebook** and **X** tabs. Previews update as you type, and the LinkedIn/Facebook previews show the actual generated card image, not a mock-up.

## Page tabs

If your product has a landing page and/or an output page, the tab shows separate configuration per page. Products that use only an AI Chat node have a single "AI Chat" set of fields for the chat's public page — this is the one that controls your `/c/…` share link.

## Setting share metadata in bulk

Connected to Claude? The connector's `update_seo` tool sets page title, meta description, share title/description (tokens supported), share image, favicon and keywords per product — so you can ask Claude to apply a share-title pattern across every product in your workspace in one conversation.

## Tips for LinkedIn

- After changing your card, run your link through the [LinkedIn Post Inspector](https://www.linkedin.com/post-inspector/) — LinkedIn caches previews for about 7 days, and the Inspector forces a fresh scrape.
- In LinkedIn **messages**, paste the link into an empty message box and wait a few seconds for the card to attach before sending (you can then delete the raw URL text — the card stays).
- Each distinct personalised URL (`?firstName=Sarah` vs `?firstName=Jordan`) is scraped separately, so personalised links don't fight each other's cache.
