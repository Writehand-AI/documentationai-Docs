The Profile page is where you manage your personal account information: your name, email address, and account preferences. These are your user-level settings, separate from workspace-level settings.

<Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The Profile settings page showing the name and email fields</Callout>

## Personal details

Update your display name and email address from the Profile page. Your name appears in the workspace interface and may appear in product outputs where a consultant name is relevant.

Your email address is used for login and for workspace notifications.

## Relationship to the Offer Profile

Your Profile page contains your personal account details. The **Offer Profile** (under Workspace Settings) is a separate, more important concept — it holds your professional positioning, methodology, and commercial guardrails. If you want to change how your expertise is presented in AI-generated outputs, go to **Workspace Settings → Offer Profile**, not this page.
