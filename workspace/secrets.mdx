title: Claude API Key
description: Run your products on your own Anthropic account, for the whole account or one workspace.
---
**Settings → AI & connections → Claude API Key** is where you add your own Anthropic (Claude) API key. It works on any plan, and only Admins can see it.

## Two places to add a key

- **Account key** — the default for every workspace on the account, whichever Admin saved it.
- **This workspace** card — a key for the workspace you're in, which takes priority over the account key. Switch workspace first to set a key for a different one.

With no key, products run on Productised's key and use your [credits](/workspace/credits).

To get a key, visit [console.anthropic.com/settings/keys](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys). Keys look like `sk-ant-...`, are encrypted before they're stored, and Productised staff can't view them.

## What you get for it

- **No credit limit.** Your plan's included leads stop being spent, so nothing counts down and nothing runs out mid-campaign.
- **Every Claude model, including Opus.** Each AI Agent node has a model picker. With your own key that choice is honoured, so you can put Opus 4.8 on your highest-stakes step. On Productised credits every node runs Sonnet 5 whatever you pick.
- **Anthropic's rates, with nothing added.** Usage is billed to your own Anthropic account at their list price. We don't re-bill it or mark it up.
- **Your own agreement with Anthropic.** The AI running your products sits under your contract — a shorter answer when a client asks who processes their data.

The lead conversation itself always runs on Claude Haiku 4.5 while it's collecting answers and Claude Sonnet 4.6 for the Advisor, whichever key is paying. The model picker applies to AI Agent nodes.

## Your key takes over straight away

A key isn't held in reserve for when your credits run out. From the moment you save it, every run it covers goes to Anthropic on that key, and your [credits](/workspace/credits) stop being spent at all — they sit unused, as a safety net for if you ever remove or rotate the key.

Anthropic bills you for that usage directly. It doesn't appear on your Productised invoice.

## What your key covers

Product conversations and results — the chat and Advisor, AI Agents, scoring, and Standard, Page and Document results — run on your Anthropic account and use no credits. So do products you build through the Claude connector.

Brain, in-app Build with AI, voice input, AI Insights and Talking Points still run on Productised's services, at no credit cost.

If your key is rejected or runs out of credit, Productised doesn't switch to its own key. Owners and Admins get an alert, and a replaced key works straight away.

## Seeing what it has cost

Your credit balance stops moving once a key is set, so it can't tell you how much you're using. **Settings → Account & billing → Usage & cost** shows what the AI cost to run in each workspace, and the Claude connector will report your runs and their cost on your own Anthropic account if you ask it about usage.

## Your Productised API key

Looking for a key to call the Productised API from your own code? That's a different key, created in **Settings → Advanced → API**. See the [API documentation](/workspace/api).
