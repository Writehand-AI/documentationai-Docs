## Who each plan is for

### Free

Free is the way to try Productised before you commit. You can build full AI Products on the canvas, get one Brain conversation a month to plan, and ship to `app.productised.ai/c/<your-product>`. What you can't do is put your own brand on the public page, point your own domain at it, or use AI Insights — those start at Starter.

If you build something with Free and want to launch it under your own brand, upgrade.

### Starter

Starter is the smallest plan that can run as your real business stack. It unlocks the things you need to ship a product professionally:

- **Custom branding** — your logo on every public product page.
- **Custom domains** — host the product at `tool.yourcompany.com` instead of the Productised default URL.
- **Page SEO** — control title, description, and social-share image for each product's public page.
- **Social post generation** — instant LinkedIn, X, and Facebook copy variants for each product.
- **5 Brain conversations a month** and **3 Journey Plans a month** — enough to plan two or three products at any one time.

Starter suits a solo founder, coach, or consultant running one or two polished AI products as a real part of their funnel — usually a top-of-funnel diagnostic and a bottom-of-funnel application form.

### Pro

Pro is for users running a portfolio of products or supporting multiple businesses. Everything in Starter, plus:

- **Unlimited Brain conversations** and **50 Journey Plans a month** — no friction when you're rethinking strategy weekly.
- **AI Insights** — automatic written summaries of patterns across all your collected responses (segment behaviour, common blockers, audience trends). See [AI Insights](/product-analytics/ai-insights).
- **Talking Points** — lead-by-lead conversation briefs generated from each response, ready to walk into a sales call with. See [Talking Points](/product-analytics/talking-points).
- **Bring Your Own Anthropic API key** — route core Claude model usage through your own Anthropic account. The product's Claude calls bill to your Anthropic account; Productised credits are reserved for Knowledge, Research, and image generation. Useful for high-volume products or teams that already have Anthropic billing in place. See [Secrets](/product-settings/secrets) and [Credits](/workspace/credits).
- **Priority support** — faster response times from the team.

Pro is the default for agencies, fractional teams, and B2B consultants running products across multiple client niches.

## Choosing between Starter and Pro

If you're not sure which to start on, ask yourself two questions:

1. **Will you analyse your audience?** AI Insights and Talking Points are Pro-only. If you're going to read every individual response yourself, Starter is fine. If you want the platform to surface patterns and conversation prep for you, you'll outgrow Starter quickly.

2. **Do you already pay for Anthropic?** BYOK is Pro-only. If you already have an Anthropic account being billed for other things, routing Productised through it can be cheaper than buying Productised credit packs at higher volume.

If both answers are no, Starter is the right home base — you can upgrade to Pro at any time without losing anything.

## Switching plans

All plan changes happen in the Stripe customer portal, accessed from **Workspace → Subscription → Manage Subscription**. See [Subscription](/workspace/subscription) for the full flow, including what happens to your live products and what's preserved if you cancel.
