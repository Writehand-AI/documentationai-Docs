Productised has three plans. The right one depends on how many products you run, how heavily you use the Brain, and whether you build for yourself or for clients.

<Callout kind="info">**Live pricing lives on [the pricing page](https://app.productised.ai/pricing).** This page describes what's *in* each plan and who it's for. Prices can change — always check the pricing page for current numbers.</Callout>

There's no free plan and no free trial. Every plan is billed monthly or annually through Stripe, and you can cancel at any time — see [Subscription](/workspace/subscription).

## Who each plan is for

### Starter

The smallest plan that runs as a real part of your business. Five AI products under your own brand and domain, with lead analytics and CRM export. Suits a coach, consultant or solo founder running a top-of-funnel diagnostic and one or two more.

### Pro

For running a portfolio. Unlimited products, three times the monthly credits, unlimited Brain conversations, and sentiment analysis on your leads. If you're regularly launching new products or rethinking your offer, Pro removes the friction.

### Agency

For building AI products **for clients** rather than for yourself. Each client gets their own [workspace](/agency/overview) — their own brand voice, branding, domain, Brain, leads and settings, with nothing shared between them.

The difference that matters: Agency includes **no AI credits**. Your products run on your own Claude API key, so you pay Anthropic directly for what your clients' products use — no credit ceiling and no markup on AI. You can also give an individual client their own key, so their usage is billed to them. See [Billing a client's AI to them](/agency/client-api-keys).

Settings → Billing → Usage & cost shows what each client's AI cost each month, side by side, with a CSV export for invoicing.

## About AI credits

Credits pay for the AI that runs your products when you're using Productised's own Claude key. Starter and Pro include a monthly allowance, and you can top up with a one-off credit pack from [Subscription](/workspace/subscription).

**If you add your own Claude API key, your products run on it instead and no credits are used** — Anthropic bills you directly. This works on every plan. See [Credits](/workspace/credits).

## Switching plans

You can move between plans from [Subscription](/workspace/subscription). Nothing you've built is deleted when you change plan.
