The Members settings page is where you manage who has access to your Productised workspace. You can invite team members, assign roles, manage permissions, and organise members into groups.

<Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The Members settings page showing the current members list and a pending invitation, with the Invite button visible</Callout>

## Inviting a team member

1. Go to **Workspace Settings → Members**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
4. Select a role for the new member
5. Send the invitation

The invited person receives an email with a link to accept and join the workspace. Until they accept, the invitation appears as **Pending** in the members list.

## Roles

Roles control what each member can do within the workspace. The available roles and their permissions are configured by the workspace owner.

Default roles include:
- **Owner** — full access to all workspace settings, products, and members
- **Admin** — can manage products and most settings, but may not be able to modify billing or ownership
- **Member** — standard access for creating and managing products

The permissions matrix is visible at the bottom of the Members page — it shows exactly which actions each role can perform across all areas of the workspace.

## Groups

You can organise members into groups for permission management at scale. Groups are particularly useful when you have team members who should share a specific set of permissions that differs from the standard roles.

Create a group, assign members to it, and configure the group's permissions from the Members settings page.

## Pending invitations

Pending invitations are listed below the active members. You can cancel a pending invitation at any time from this list. If the invited person does not receive the email, ask them to check spam — or cancel and re-invite.

## Removing a member

To remove a member from the workspace, use the action menu on their row in the members list. Removed members lose immediate access to the workspace and all products within it.
