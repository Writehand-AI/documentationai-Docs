Productised keeps you in the loop two ways: an **in-app notification centre** that's always in your top bar, and optional **email lead alerts** you can switch on per product. Nothing about either is visible to your respondents.

## The notification centre (in-app)

The **bell icon** in the top navigation is your notification centre. Open it to see recent activity across your workspace — new leads, response milestones, low AI credits, API-key changes, and more. Unread items show a badge on the bell.

**Manage what you get:** open the bell, then the **gear icon → Notification Preferences**. Each notification type (for example, **New Product Response** and **First Response Milestone**) can be turned on or off individually.

<Callout kind="info">
The notification centre works out of the box — there's nothing to set up. It's the fastest way to glance at what's happened while you were away.
</Callout>

## Email lead notifications

Want the new-lead hit to land in your inbox too? Turn on **Lead Notifications** for a product and Productised emails you every time someone completes it.

### Turn it on

1. Open the product → **Settings → Deployment**.
2. Toggle **Lead Notifications** on.
3. Save.

<Callout kind="info">
**Off by default, and set per product.** You opt in only for the products you actually want inbox pings for — a lead magnet you're actively watching, say, and not an evergreen one you check weekly. Everyone in the workspace who administers it receives the email.
</Callout>

### What the email contains

Each email is a quick, scannable summary — not a data dump:

- **Name, email, and status** of the person who completed the product
- **A link** straight to that lead in your responses
- **The AI score and segment**, when the product scores its leads — and a **high-scoring lead is flagged as 🔥 hot**, so the ones worth calling first jump out of your inbox

The full answers and AI result stay in the app, behind your login — the email is just the nudge to go look.

### Good to know

- It fires **once per completed lead** — not on every message as they fill it in.
- It never affects the respondent's experience, and a delivery hiccup never blocks a lead from being captured.
- To stop them, toggle **Lead Notifications** back off on that product (there's also a one-tap link at the bottom of every email).

<Callout kind="tip">
Want leads to flow into your CRM or other tools as well? That's separate from these alerts — see [Connect your CRM](/integrations/connect-your-crm) for webhooks, Zapier, and more.
</Callout>
