Productised keeps you in the loop two ways: an **in-app notification centre** in your top bar, and optional **lead emails** you can switch on per product. Nothing about either is visible to your respondents.

## The notification centre (in-app)

The **bell icon** in the top navigation is your notification centre. Owners and Admins see activity across the account there — including each lead that **completes** a product, response milestones, low credits, API key changes, and alerts when a product's AI fails. Unread items show a badge on the bell.

**Manage what you get:** open the bell, then the **gear icon → Notification Preferences**, and switch each notification type on or off.

<Callout kind="info">
The notification centre is for Owners and Admins. Members don't receive lead notifications in the bell.
</Callout>

## Lead emails

Want new leads to land in your inbox too? Turn on **Lead Notifications** for a product and Productised emails you each time someone completes it.

### Turn it on

1. Open the product → **Settings → Deployment**.
2. Switch **Lead Notifications** on. It saves straight away.

<Callout kind="info">
**Off by default, and set per product.** You opt in only for the products you want emails for. The email goes to the workspace's owner and the people added to that workspace (up to 10). If nobody is added to the workspace, it goes to everyone on the account.
</Callout>

### What the email contains

Each email is a quick, scannable summary:

- **Name, email and status** of the person who completed the product
- **The AI score and segment**, when the product scores its leads — and a **high-scoring lead is flagged as 🔥 hot**, so the ones worth calling first stand out
- **A link** to the product's analytics, where you'll find the lead

The full answers and AI result stay in the app, behind your login — the email is just the nudge to go and look.

### Good to know

- It's sent **once per completed lead** — not on every message, and not for people who leave before finishing.
- It never affects the respondent's experience, and a delivery hiccup never stops a lead being captured.
- To stop them, switch **Lead Notifications** off on that product. The **Turn these off** link at the bottom of every email takes you to the product's settings.

<Callout kind="tip">
Want leads to flow into your CRM or other tools as well? That's separate from these alerts — see [Connect your CRM](/integrations/connect-your-crm) for webhooks, Zapier, and more.
</Callout>
