The Subscription page is where you manage your plan, view invoices, and update payment methods. Billing is handled through Stripe.

<Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** The Subscription settings page showing the current plan, billing cycle, and the Manage Subscription button</Callout>

## Current plan

Your active plan and billing cycle are shown at the top of the page. Productised plans differ in the number of live products you can have, feature availability (custom branding, custom domains, BYOK, advanced analytics), and model credit allocations.

## Managing your subscription

Click **Manage Subscription** to open the Stripe customer portal. From there you can:

- Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- Update your payment method
- View and download past invoices
- Cancel your subscription

Changes to your plan take effect at the start of the next billing cycle, unless you are upgrading — upgrades take effect immediately with prorated charges.

## Payment methods

Payment method management is handled in the Stripe customer portal. Productised does not store card details.

## Cancellation

You can cancel at any time from the Stripe customer portal. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. After cancellation, your workspace and products are retained but features may be restricted based on the free tier limits.
