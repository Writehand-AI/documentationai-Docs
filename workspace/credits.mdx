title: Credits

---
Credits pay for the AI when Productised's own Claude key runs your products. If you add your own Claude key, your products don't use credits at all.

## What uses credits

**Each visitor conversation uses 1 credit**, taken when the visitor sends their first message. It's the same single credit however long the conversation is and whatever result it generates.

These don't use credits:

- Building products — in the app, with Build with AI, or through the Claude connector
- Brain (it has its own monthly conversation limit on each plan)
- Any conversation run on your own Claude key

## Your allowance

Your plan includes a credit allowance that resets at the start of each billing period. If you use it up, you can buy add-on credits or upgrade your plan.

## Where to see your balance

Open **Settings → Account & billing → Subscription** and choose the **Usage** tab. The **Credits** tab on the same page is where you buy add-on credits.

**Settings → Account & billing → Usage & cost** shows what the AI cost to run in each workspace, and how many credits each one used.

## Bring your own key

You can run your products on your own Claude API key on **any plan**. An Admin adds it in **Settings → AI & connections → Claude API Key**.

Keys can be set at two levels, and the more specific one wins:

1. **A workspace's key** — for every product in that workspace; useful on the [Agency plan](/agency/client-api-keys) to bill a client directly
2. **Your account key** — the default for every other workspace

With no key set, products run on Productised's key and use your credits.

With your own key, product conversations and results are billed to your Anthropic account. Brain, Build with AI, voice input, AI Insights and Talking Points still run on Productised's services, at no credit cost. See [AI keys](/product-settings/secrets).
