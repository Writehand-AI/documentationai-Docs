The **Advisor** outcome (the *Advisor* / advisor_chat node) turns the product into a two-phase experience. First the AI collects what it needs; then, instead of ending with a static result, it **continues the conversation as a live advisor** — speaking first with full context from everything the person shared, then answering their follow-up questions.

It's the closest thing to a personalised consultation that runs itself.

## When to use it

- **Coaching or consultation intake** — collect the situation, then advise on it live.
- **Discovery-style products** — qualify and guide a prospect toward booking.
- **Any product where the value is the back-and-forth**, not a fixed deliverable.

## How it works

1. Add an **Advisor** node to the canvas and connect it below your AI Product node.
2. Give it a **persona / system prompt** — who the advisor is, how it should behave, and what it should steer toward (e.g. booking a call).
3. Publish. After collection, the AI opens the advisory phase automatically, grounded in the respondent's answers.

<Callout kind="tip">Pair the Advisor with a clear CTA in its instructions — e.g. guide the conversation toward *"book a strategy call"* — so the live advice leads somewhere.</Callout>

## Advisor vs the other outcomes

Where [Standard](/outcomes/standard) and [Page](/outcomes/assessments) deliver a *result*, the Advisor delivers a *conversation*. Choose it when the ongoing dialogue is the product.
