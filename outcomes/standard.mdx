## What Standard does

The Standard outcome streams a fully AI-generated, personalised output at the end of the conversation. The moment the collection phase ends, the AI begins writing — and your end user watches their personalised result appear in real time.

No two outputs are the same, because no two conversations are the same. The AI uses everything it collected to produce something that reflects that specific person's situation.

## When to use Standard

Standard is the right outcome for most products:

- Lead qualification and diagnostic products where the output is a personalised analysis
- Scorecard products where the result breaks down their situation across multiple dimensions
- Any product where you want the output delivered instantly, without a separate designed page
- First-impression products where the streamed result itself is part of the value

Use **Page** instead when you need a fully branded, custom HTML layout. Use **Advisor** when you want the AI to continue the conversation interactively after collection.

## How to configure it

Click the Standard node on the canvas to open its settings in the right panel.

- **Custom Instructions** — the most important setting. Tell the AI how to write this specific output: what structure to follow, what to emphasise, what tone to use, what call to action to include. The more specific, the better.
- **Tone** — a default tone preset the AI follows when generating content.

The AI's system prompt, the collection goals, and the Custom Instructions all combine to shape what gets written. Custom Instructions is where you direct the output format and intent.

## An idea to get you started

A lead qualification product with Standard output: someone answers six questions about their business situation. Your Standard node has Custom Instructions telling the AI to structure the output as: a one-paragraph summary of their situation, three priority gaps identified, and a specific next step. The AI writes all of it, personalised, in under ten seconds.

That output is the thing that makes your end user think: "this person really understands my situation." Done well, it replaces the discovery call.
