The **Standard** outcome (the *Standard* / doc_builder node) has the AI write a personalised written result and stream it directly into the product the moment collection finishes. It's the fastest outcome to set up and the right default for most products.

Think reports, plans, diagnoses, summaries, action lists — anything where a well-written, personalised piece of text is the deliverable.

## How it works

1. Add a **Standard** node to the canvas and connect it below your AI Product node.
2. In its settings, give the AI **custom instructions** — what the result should cover, its structure, and its tone.
3. Publish. Every respondent gets their own version, written live from their answers.

The AI writes and formats the content itself, guided by your instructions and the full conversation context. There's no HTML to design — that's what makes it fast.

## Standard vs Page

| | Standard | [Page](/outcomes/assessments) |
|---|---|---|
| **How it's produced** | AI writes and formats the text directly | You design custom HTML in Claude; tokens fill it per respondent |
| **Visual control** | Text-based | Complete — any layout, charts, branding |
| **Best for** | Most products — fast, instant, personalised | Premium scorecards and branded visual results |
| **Setup** | Custom instructions only | Design the page in Claude |

Use **Standard** when you want the AI to write and deliver the result without a custom design. Use **Page** when visual control matters.

## Editing via Claude

With the [Claude connector](/claude-connector/overview), you can tune a Standard outcome conversationally — *"make my report shorter and add a section on next steps"* — and Claude updates the node's instructions for you.
