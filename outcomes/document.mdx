## What a Document is

A Document is a structured, formatted output — more formal than a Report, designed to be downloaded, shared, or used as a reference. Think of it as the thing you would normally spend hours producing after a consultation: a 90-day action plan, a strategy brief, a personalised audit summary, a client-ready proposal, a resource guide with your methodology baked in.

Every Document is AI-generated from the conversation data. No two documents are the same — the AI uses what the person told you to personalise every section, grounding the content in your framework and methodology, writing in the tone you specify.

## Report vs Document — when to use which

**Report** is insight-led and narrative. It streams in real time, uses visual components (score gauges, charts, cards), and is best for first impressions, diagnostics, and lead generation moments where the experience itself is part of the value.

**Document** is structured and formal. It is best for client deliverables, proposals, plans, and anything someone will save, reference, or share with someone else. If the person would print it and bring it to a meeting, it should be a Document.

## Document types

When you configure the Document node, you choose from six document types. Each type comes with a default section structure that the AI uses as a starting point:

| Type                | Best for                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Auto**            | When you want Productised to infer the right structure from the conversation |
| **Action Plan**     | Implementation plans, consultant next steps, delivery handoffs               |
| **Strategy Brief**  | Executive summaries, consultant recaps, strategic recommendations            |
| **Resource Guide**  | Lead magnets, educational guides, evergreen advisory content                 |
| **Analysis Report** | Diagnostics, score-based reviews, evidence-backed assessments                |
| **Proposal**        | Sales follow-up, solution proposals, commercial recommendation documents     |

## Sidebar settings

When you click the Document node on the canvas, the sidebar opens with three sections: **Content**, **Design**, and **Call to Action**. Use the **Expand** button to open the live document preview.

**Content section**

- **Document type** — choose from Auto, Action Plan, Strategy Brief, Resource Guide, Analysis Report, or Proposal. Each type sets the default section structure.

- **Title** — optional. Leave blank and the AI generates a personalised title based on the person's situation.

- **Number of Sections** — a slider from 2 to 5. Controls how many sections the document will contain. Overridden if you define custom section names.

- **Section Length** — Brief (\~250 words per section), Standard (\~450 words), or Detailed (\~700 words). Match this to how much depth the outcome should carry.

- **Section Structure** — optionally define custom section names, in order. When section names are specified, they override the slider count and the AI uses your exact headings.

- **Tone** — Consultative (warm, expert advisory), Formal (professional, authoritative), Casual (friendly and approachable), or Technical (detailed and data-driven).

- **Custom Instructions** — optional additional guidance for the AI about how to write this document, what to emphasise, or what to avoid.

**Design section**

- **Presentation Pack** — choose from Nova, Editorial, Boardroom, and Signal. Each pack changes the visual language: typography, layout density, colour application, and section styling.

- **Accent Colour** — the brand colour applied to section headings and decorative elements.

- **Font** — set a typeface for the document body.

- **Page Width** — Narrow (720px), Medium (900px), or Wide (1100px).

- **PDF Download** — toggle on or off to show or hide a download button for end users.

**Call to Action section**

- **Button Text** — optional CTA button label (e.g. "Book a Strategy Call").

- **Button URL** — the destination URL for the CTA button.

- **Button Colours** — background and text colour for the CTA button.

**Locked Content section**

- Locked content blocks are builder-owned content that always appears in the document, regardless of what the AI generates. You can add headings, paragraphs, and images, and position them anywhere in the document: after the cover, before or after any section, or at the end. Use locked content for branding elements, boilerplate text, or fixed calls to action that should never be AI-generated.

## Expanded view

Click the **Expand** button to open the Document's live preview overlay. This is a full-screen view of the document as it will appear to the end user, rendered with your current configuration and placeholder data.

The preview shows:

- The document cover with the title and label

- All sections with placeholder-length content, styled in the selected presentation pack

- Any locked content blocks you have configured, positioned correctly

- The CTA button if configured

- The disclaimer if set

The document type and the number of locked content blocks are shown in the header. Press Escape to close the preview.

Use the expanded view to validate the document's structure, check the visual presentation, and confirm that locked content blocks are positioned correctly before publishing your product.

## When to use Document

- Any product where the output is something the client will use, keep, or share — not just read and close

- Post-consultation deliverables you want to automate: action plans, strategy briefs, audit summaries

- High-ticket lead gen where the freebie is a personalised document that demonstrates your methodology

- Client onboarding products that generate a personalised starting brief from intake questions

## An idea to get you started

Use a Document for a personalised 90-day business plan. Someone answers eight questions about their goals, current situation, and biggest obstacles. Your AI product generates a formatted, structured Action Plan — in your voice, grounded in your framework, with their specific situation woven through every section. That is a lead magnet that closes.

The document exists as a real file in the world, not a result screen inside a platform. It gets downloaded. It gets opened in meetings. It travels.
