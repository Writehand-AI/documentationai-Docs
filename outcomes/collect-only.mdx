title: Collect only
description: Save the respondent's answers with no AI output at the end — for waitlists, applications, and simple intake.
---

**Collect only** is the simplest outcome: the product runs the conversation, saves the answers as a lead, and ends there — with **no AI-generated result** shown to the respondent. It's what you have when an AI Product node has no Outcome node attached.

## When to use it

- **Waitlists and applications** — you want the submission, not a deliverable.
- **Simple intake forms** — collect details before a call or onboarding.
- **A first step** — launch fast to start capturing leads, then add a [Standard](/outcomes/standard), [Advisor](/outcomes/advisor), or [Page](/outcomes/assessments) outcome later to give respondents something back.

## How it works

1. Build your AI Product node with the questions you want to collect.
2. Leave it without an Outcome node (or choose *Collect only* when creating the product).
3. Publish. Every completed conversation is saved as a lead in [Results](/product-analytics/viewing-results) — name, email, and every answer — ready for follow-up, export, or your CRM via [Connections](/integrations/connections) and [Webhooks](/integrations/webhooks).

<Callout kind="tip">Even with no on-screen result, the full answer set is captured — so a Collect-only product is still a complete lead-capture tool. Add an outcome any time to upgrade the experience without losing data.</Callout>
