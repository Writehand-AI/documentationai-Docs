---
title: What are Outcomes?
description: An Outcome is what your end user receives at the end of a product — personalised, and built from what they told you.
---

An **Outcome** is the deliverable at the end of every AI product. Once the conversation is complete and the AI has what it needs, it produces the Outcome — something specific to that person, built from their answers.

This is what separates Productised from a lead form or a quiz: your end user doesn't get a generic results page, they get something that reflects their situation.

## The four Outcome types

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="Standard" href="/outcomes/standard" icon="file-text" horizontal="false">
    The AI streams a personalised written result straight into the product — a report, plan, or analysis. The fastest way to deliver a personalised result, and the default for most products.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Advisor" href="/outcomes/advisor" icon="message-circle" horizontal="false">
    After collecting answers, the AI continues as a live personalised advisor in the same chat — great for coaching intake and consultation-style products.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Page" href="/outcomes/assessments" icon="code" horizontal="false">
    A fully custom HTML results page, generated per respondent at a shareable URL — scores, charts, branded layout. Designed in Claude via the connector. Best for premium, visual deliverables like scorecards.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect only" href="/outcomes/collect-only" icon="inbox" horizontal="false">
    The answers are saved with no AI output at the end — for waitlists, applications, and simple intake. Add an Outcome later to turn it into a full experience.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How Outcomes connect on the canvas

Each Outcome is a node you add from the canvas toolbar and connect below your **AI Product** node. Most products route through an **AI Agent** node first — it analyses the collected answers and produces the structured outputs (scores, tiers, insights) that the Outcome then presents. See [Connecting Powers and Outcomes](/canvas/connecting-powers-and-outcomes).

## What makes an Outcome personalised

The AI has the full conversation in context when it produces the Outcome — every answer, every nuance. A Standard result for a founder who said they're pre-revenue reads differently from one for a founder with an established product. Not because you wrote two versions, but because the AI understood the difference.

<Callout kind="tip">Your AI Agent assigns each user a named tier (e.g. "Growth-Ready", "Not Yet Ready") as it scores them. Give the agent guidance on how each tier's result should differ, and the Outcome adapts its content and CTA to that tier — no manual routing rules needed.</Callout>
