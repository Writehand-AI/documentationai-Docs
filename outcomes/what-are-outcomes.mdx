---
title: What are Outcomes?
description: Outcomes are what your end users receive at the end of your AI product — personalised, and built from what they told you.
---

An Outcome is the deliverable at the end of every AI product. Once the conversation is complete and the AI has collected everything it needs, it generates the Outcome — something specific to that person, built from what they shared.

This is what separates Productised from a lead form or a quiz. Your end user does not receive a generic results page. They receive something that reflects their situation.

## The Outcome types

<Columns cols="3">
  <Card title="Standard" href="/outcomes/standard" icon="file-text" horizontal="false">
    The AI streams a personalised report or document directly in the product. Clean, readable, and instant. The default output for most products.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Page" href="/canvas/design-node" icon="layout" horizontal="false">
    A fully custom HTML result page you design with Claude. Uses live tokens to pull in AI outputs and form answers. Best for branded, visual experiences.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Advisor" href="/canvas/advisor-chat-node" icon="message-circle" horizontal="false">
    After collection ends, the AI shifts into a live advisory session — speaking first with everything it learned, then opening the floor for follow-up questions.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How Outcomes connect to your AI Product

Every Outcome is a node on the canvas. You drag it from the toolbar, drop it below your AI Product node, and draw a connection between them. The Outcome you choose must match the **After collection** setting in your AI Product's Behaviour tab.

See [Building your canvas](/canvas/connecting-powers-and-outcomes) for the step-by-step.

## What makes an Outcome personalised

The AI has the full conversation in context when it generates the Outcome — every answer, every nuance of what the person shared. It uses this to make the output specific.

A Standard output generated for a founder who said they're pre-revenue looks different from one generated for a founder with an established product. Not because you wrote two versions — because the AI understood the difference and responded to it.

This is the core of what Productised does: every Outcome is different because every conversation is different.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Your AI Agent assigns each user a named tier (e.g. "Growth-Ready", "Not Yet Ready") as it scores them. Give the agent guidance on how each tier's result should differ, and the Outcome adapts its content and CTA to that tier — no manual routing rules needed.
</Callout>
