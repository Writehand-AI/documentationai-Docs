---
title: What are Outcomes?
description: Outcomes are what your audience receives at the end of your AI product — tangible, personalised, and built from what they told you.
---

An Outcome is the deliverable at the end of every AI product. Once the conversation is complete and the AI has collected everything it needs, it generates the Outcome — something specific to that person, built from what they shared.

This is what separates Productised from a lead form or a quiz. The person does not receive a generic results page. They receive something that reflects their situation — a report, a document, or a redirect to the right next step.

## The Outcome types

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="Report" href="/outcomes/report" icon="file-text" horizontal="false">
    The AI generates a personalised diagnostic report — sections, scores, insights, and a call to action. Instantly delivered. Visually rich.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Document" href="/outcomes/document" icon="file" horizontal="false">
    The AI writes a full personalised document — an action plan, strategy brief, proposal, or guide — that the person can view and download.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Advisor Chat" href="/canvas/advisor-chat-node" icon="message-circle" horizontal="false">
    A full-screen, personalised advisory session. The AI speaks first using everything it collected, then opens the floor for follow-up questions. The most conversational Outcome type.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Redirect" href="/outcomes/redirect" icon="external-link" horizontal="false">
    Route different people to different URLs based on their result. Send high-fit leads to your booking page, lower-fit leads to a different resource.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How Outcomes connect to your AI Product

Every Outcome is a node on the canvas. You drag it from the toolbar, drop it below your AI Product node, and draw a connection between them. The Outcome you choose must match the **After collection** setting in your AI Product's Behaviour tab.

See [Connecting Powers and Outcomes](/canvas/connecting-powers-and-outcomes) for the step-by-step.

## What makes an Outcome personalised

The AI has the full conversation in context when it generates the Outcome — every answer, every score, every nuance of what the person shared. It uses this to make the output specific.

A Report generated for a founder who said they're pre-revenue looks different from one generated for a founder with an established product. Not because you wrote two versions — because the AI understood the difference and responded to it.

This is the core of what Productised does: every Outcome is different because every conversation is different.

<Callout kind="tip">
Your AI Agent assigns each respondent a named tier (e.g. "Growth-Ready", "Not Yet Ready") as it scores them. Give the agent guidance on how each tier's result should differ, and the Outcome adapts its content and CTA to that tier — no manual routing rules on your part.
</Callout>
