Most platforms stop at the result screen. A score. A label. A paragraph of generic advice. Productised starts there.

An Outcome is the tangible thing your audience walks away with — something they save, act on, or share. Not a dashboard metric that disappears when they close the tab. Not a platform artifact that only exists inside the tool. A real deliverable: a formatted report, a downloadable document, a personalised commercial recommendation, a link to exactly the right next step.

## Why Outcomes are what they are

In professional services, the quality of your output is the quality of your reputation.

When someone goes through your AI product and receives a report that feels like it was written specifically for them — with their language, their situation, their next step — that is not just a good user experience. That is a conversion. That is someone thinking: "this person really understands me." And that thought is what moves a stranger into a client.

Outcomes were not designed because they were easy to build. They were designed because they are what actually moves people to action. Every Outcome type in Productised maps to a natural endpoint of a professional conversation — the kind of thing you would produce after a discovery call, a consultation, or an introductory meeting.

## How Outcomes connect to the decision model

The AI's decision model — how it evaluated the person during collection — directly shapes the Outcome. The same product can generate a completely different report, document, or offer depending on who went through it.

A high-fit lead receives an Offer with a strong, specific CTA. A medium-fit lead receives a Report with a more diagnostic frame and a softer next step. A lower-fit lead gets redirected to a resource that matches their current stage. None of this requires you to set up complex routing rules. The AI understands what each person needs based on the decision result, and shapes the Outcome accordingly.

## The Outcomes library is growing

This is a core part of Productised's roadmap. As we learn more about how coaches and consultants deliver value, we add new Outcome types that match. The goal is for every natural endpoint of a professional conversation to be available as an Outcome — so that whatever you would normally deliver after a great discovery call, your AI product can deliver at scale.

## The bigger picture

AI agents are now available to anyone. AI tools are everywhere. But an AI that generates a beautifully formatted, personally relevant report — one that positions you as the expert and ends with a clear next step tailored to that person — that is not a tool. That is a product. And that is the difference.
