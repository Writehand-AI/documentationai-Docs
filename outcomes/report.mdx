## What a Report is

A Report is a fully AI-generated, personalised insight document delivered at the end of the conversation. No two reports are the same — because no two conversations are the same. The AI uses everything it collected, every answer, every nuance, to produce something that feels like it was written specifically for that person.

The Report streams in real time. The moment the conversation ends, the AI begins generating — and the person watches their personalised report appear, section by section. The experience itself is part of the value.

## This is your first impression at scale

For most coaches and consultants, the Report is the thing that makes a stranger think: "this person really understands my situation."

Done well, it replaces the need for a discovery call. Done exceptionally, it creates demand before you have spoken a word. The person who goes through your product and receives a report that names their specific situation, identifies the gap they knew existed but could not quite articulate, and points to a clear next step — that person is already converted. All that remains is the conversation.

## How the AI builds the report

The report is shaped by four inputs working together:

1. **The AI Product's system prompt** — your expert framework and how the AI should approach the person's situation
2. **The collection goals** — what was asked during the conversation, and what each response means
3. **The decision model result** — how the person was evaluated: their overall status, their top strengths, their priority gaps
4. **The Offer Profile** — your commercial positioning, your CTA defaults, your approved claims

All of this combines to produce an outcome that is personalised to the individual and grounded in your expertise. The AI does not generate generic advice — it generates an analysis of this person's specific situation through the lens of your methodology.

## Sidebar settings

When you click the Report node on the canvas, the sidebar opens with three tabs: **Content**, **Design**, and **Behaviour**. Use the **Expand** button to open the full preview.

**Content tab**

- **Report Skill** — choose the analytical lens the AI applies to the collected data. Skills set default tone, output structure, and weighting rules. Available skills include Diagnostic Report, Scorecard Analysis, Strategy Briefing, and others. Selecting a skill presets the archetype and presentation pack, which you can override.
- **Report Archetype** — the structural shape of the report (e.g. diagnostic, scorecard, strategy). This determines which sections the AI generates and how they are organised.
- **Custom Instructions** — optional guidance to the AI about how this specific report should be written, what to emphasise, what to avoid, or how to frame the recommendations.
- **Per-section Guidance** — optional directional prompts for individual report sections (Verdict, Breakdown, Insight Cards, Next Steps). Use these when you want the AI to approach a specific section in a particular way.
- **CTA configuration** — CTA button text, URL, body text, background colour, and font colour. These default to the values in your Offer Profile if left blank.
- **Name field / Company field** — specify which collected fields contain the respondent's name and company, so the report can address them personally.
- **Logo** — upload a logo to appear in the report header.

**Design tab**

- **Presentation Pack** — choose from Nova, Editorial, Boardroom, and Signal. Each pack changes the visual language: typography, colour application, layout density, and section styling.
- **Primary Colour** — the brand colour applied to headings, accent lines, and the CTA button.
- **Page Width** — Narrow (720px), Medium (900px), or Wide (1100px).
- **Page Background** — set a background colour or upload a background image.
- **Heading Font / Body Font** — set custom typefaces for the report.
- **Include Charts / Include Cards / Include Tables** — toggle specific report components on or off. Charts visualise score data. Cards surface key findings. Tables provide structured comparisons.

**Behaviour tab**

- **Max Follow-up Messages** — after the report generates, users can continue the conversation with the AI as an advisor. This setting limits how many follow-up messages are allowed.
- **Linked Products** — optionally link to other products in your workspace that should be presented as next steps or alternatives in the report.

## Expanded view

Click the **Expand** button in the sidebar to open the Report's expanded view — a full-screen, two-panel interface with a live preview of the report on the left and live controls on the right.

**Preview panel (left)** — a live render of your report using sample data and your current configuration. Shows exactly how the report will look to your audience, including fonts, colours, section layout, score visualisations, and the CTA. Toggle between desktop and mobile viewports using the icons in the header.

**Controls panel (right)** — live configuration changes that update the preview instantly:
- **Presentation Pack switcher** — click between packs to see the design change in real time
- **Component toggles** — toggle charts, distribution charts, cards, and tables on or off and watch the preview update
- **Section visibility** — show or hide individual sections of the report

Changes made in the expanded view write back to the node configuration immediately.

## When to use a Report

- Lead qualification products where the report frames your offer and positions you as the expert
- Diagnostic tools where the output is a personalised analysis of the person's situation
- Scorecard products where the report breaks down their results across multiple dimensions and recommends a next step
- Any product where the first impression needs to do real work — where the output itself is part of the conversion

## A note on quality

Your report is a permanent record of how good you are. It gets saved, shared, and referenced. People come back to it. They quote it in conversations with colleagues. They show it to partners.

Make it good enough that that happens. The bar is: does this feel like it was written for me specifically? Does it say something I did not already know but immediately recognise as true? Does it point me toward a next step I actually want to take? If yes to all three, your report is doing its job.
