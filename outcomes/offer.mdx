## What an Offer is

An Offer is the conversion moment. After the AI has collected everything it needs, it generates a personalised commercial recommendation — not a generic pitch, but a tailored presentation of how you can help this specific person, based on exactly what they shared.

The Offer takes everything the conversation revealed — their situation, their goals, their challenges, their readiness — and presents your service as the obvious next step for them specifically. It is not a one-size-fits-all sales page. It is a recommendation that feels like it was written after a consultation, because it was — just not one that required your time.

## This is not a sales page

A sales page says the same thing to everyone. An Offer says something different to each person — because it is built from their conversation. Their situation. Their fit score. Their goals.

The AI knows what they need. The Offer presents it.

When someone goes through a well-designed Offer product, they receive something that feels like the commercial part of a discovery call — except it was generated instantly, it references things they actually said, and it ends with a step they are already primed to take.

## What an Offer contains

Every Offer is structured around a set of core sections, all generated by the AI from the collected data and your commercial configuration:

- **Title and label** — the headline and offer category label (e.g. "Recommended Offer", "Package Match")
- **Hero summary** — an opening paragraph that frames the recommendation in the context of what the person shared
- **Why this fits** — a personalised explanation of why this specific offer is right for this specific person. This is where the AI draws on the conversation data to make the recommendation feel earned, not generic.
- **The offer** — the offer name, a pricing label (if configured), a summary of what is included, and a description of the pricing detail
- **What's included** — a list of the items included in the offer, drawn from your configured inclusions
- **What happens next** — a list of next steps that follows naturally from accepting the offer
- **CTA** — the call to action button, with your configured label and URL
- **Alternatives** (optional) — if enabled and relevant, the AI can include alternative options when the primary offer is not the only reasonable fit
- **FAQ** (optional) — concise answers to common objections that the AI determines would reduce hesitation for this specific person
- **Disclaimer** (optional) — any legal or commercial disclaimer that should appear at the end

## How it draws from the Offer Profile

The Offer uses your Offer Profile (set in workspace settings) as its commercial foundation. Your Offer Profile contains:

- Your default CTA label and URL — used when the Offer node does not override them
- Your approved claims, positioning statements, and methodology references
- Your default disclaimer

This means every Offer your products generate stays on-brand and within the guardrails you have set. The AI never invents commercial terms, certifications, guarantees, or pricing outside what you have explicitly configured.

## How the decision model shapes the Offer

The AI's decision model result — high, medium, or low fit — directly influences how the Offer is presented.

A high-fit result produces an Offer with a stronger, more confident fit summary and a direct CTA. A medium-fit result produces a more exploratory framing, with the fit rationale acknowledging some open questions. A lower-fit result may surface an alternative path or a softer next step.

None of this requires manual routing rules. The AI understands the decision result and frames the Offer accordingly.

## Sidebar settings

When you click the Offer node on the canvas, the sidebar opens with four sections: **Offer Setup**, **Pricing**, **What's Included**, and **CTA and Presentation**.

**Offer Setup**

- **Offer type** — choose from four types:
  - *Offer* — best next commercial recommendation (default)
  - *Package Match* — recommend the best-fit package or tier
  - *Quote Estimate* — present a personalised estimate or range
  - *Strategy Call* — qualify the lead into a booked next step
- **Offer title** — optional. Leave blank and the AI generates a personalised title.
- **Offer brief** — custom instructions for the AI. Use this to explain what should be emphasised in the offer, what kind of lead it is designed for, and what the AI should avoid.

**Pricing**

Choose a pricing mode:
- *Hide price* — CTA-led, no price revealed. Best when pricing depends on scope or when you want to qualify before disclosing.
- *Fixed price* — shows one exact investment (e.g. $3,000). The AI uses this price verbatim.
- *Price range* — shows a low-to-high expected range. The AI uses only the configured range.
- *Custom text* — wording like "Investment confirmed after a short fit call". The AI uses this wording exactly.

**What's Included**

Add up to eight bullet-point inclusions. The AI can reuse or adapt these per person, but these are the grounded commercial truths it starts from. You can also paste an existing offer document or proposal language — the AI uses it as reference material rather than inventing from scratch.

**CTA and Presentation**

- **CTA text and URL** — override the Offer Profile defaults for this specific Offer node. Leave blank to use the profile defaults.
- **Show alternatives** — enable to allow the AI to include alternative options when relevant.
- **Presentation pack** — Nova, Editorial, Boardroom, or Signal. Controls the visual language of the rendered Offer.
- **Page width** — Narrow, Medium, or Wide.
- **Primary colour** — brand colour applied to the Offer's visual elements.
- **Font** — typeface for the Offer document.

## When to use an Offer

- Any lead generation product where the goal is to convert the audience into a paying client
- Products where you want to present your service to people who have already shown you their situation
- High-intent funnels where the AI has enough collected data to make a genuine, personalised recommendation
- Products where you want the commercial recommendation to feel earned rather than scripted

## Connecting Offer to your Offer Profile

The Offer outcome draws its commercial foundation from your Offer Profile in workspace settings. Review your [Offer Profile](/workspace/offer-profile) to ensure your positioning, CTA defaults, and approved claims are configured before you publish an Offer product.

## An idea to get you started

Combine an Offer with the decision model: high-fit leads receive a personalised offer for your flagship programme, complete with a "why this fits" section that references what they shared. Everyone else sees a redirect to a lower-ticket entry point. Both experiences feel personal. Neither feels like a funnel — because the AI is doing the thinking, not a set of if-then rules you manually configured.
