The expertise you've spent years developing — your frameworks, your models, your methodology — doesn't have to live only in your head, your courses, or your one-to-one sessions. Productised lets you package it into structured learning experiences that adapt to each person who goes through them.

These are not courses. They are not generic quizzes. They are AI-powered experiences that draw on your specific knowledge, meet each person where they are, and deliver something personalised and actionable.

<Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** A training product interface — showing a conversational question about a specific learning topic with a branded background</Callout>

## How it works

A training or learning product on Productised uses the AI conversation to assess where someone currently is — their knowledge level, their specific challenges, their learning goals. The AI evaluates their responses and generates an outcome tailored to their situation:

- A **report** that identifies their key development areas and a personalised learning path
- A **document** that summarises the key concepts relevant to their situation, in the context of your framework
- A **redirect** to a specific module, resource, or next step that matches their profile

The Knowledge Power makes this particularly powerful: upload your training materials, your framework documents, or your methodology guides, and the AI draws on them when generating the personalised output. The result reflects your thinking, not generic AI advice.

## The broader opportunity

Training and learning products sit at an interesting intersection: they deliver genuine value to the person going through them, while simultaneously demonstrating your expertise to anyone who hasn't yet hired you.

A well-built learning product is both a client tool and a lead generation asset. Someone goes through your diagnostic, receives a personalised learning plan built on your framework, and immediately understands both the depth of your thinking and the value of working with you directly. That's a conversion that happens without a sales conversation.
