The first session with a new client is often spent catching up: understanding their situation, establishing their goals, identifying the gaps. It's valuable time, but it doesn't have to work that hard if you've done the groundwork first.

A Productised client onboarding product does that groundwork automatically. Before your client ever books time with you, they go through a structured AI conversation that collects everything you need to know — and delivers something back to them that makes the engagement feel premium from day one.

<Callout kind="info">📸 **Screenshot needed:** An onboarding product interface showing a welcome screen with the coach's branding, and the first contextual question visible</Callout>

## How it works

Your client receives a link before their first session. They go through your onboarding product — answering questions about their goals, current situation, challenges, and context. The AI collects what it needs, evaluates their responses against your framework, and produces a structured output:

- A **document** that summarises their situation and goals, formatted in your style — ready for you to review before the call
- A **report** that gives them a personalised overview of where they stand and what the engagement will focus on
- A **redirect** to a prep guide or welcome resource tailored to their profile

You arrive at the first session already knowing who you're talking to. They arrive feeling like you've already done the work.

## Why this matters

Client onboarding products are a subtle but significant signal to your clients about how you work. A generic intake form says "we need information." A personalised AI product that delivers a thoughtful summary back to them before session one says: "we're already thinking about your situation."

That's a positioning advantage that compounds over time. Clients who arrive prepared get better outcomes. Better outcomes become testimonials. Testimonials become the proof that fills your next cohort.
