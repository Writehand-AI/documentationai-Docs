<Callout kind="info">
**Knowledge Power vs Brain Knowledge Base** — there are two places knowledge lives in Productised and they serve different purposes. The Knowledge Power (this page) is attached to a specific product on the canvas — it grounds that product's AI conversations and outputs in your documents. [Brain's Knowledge Base](/brain/knowledge-bases) is workspace-wide — it feeds Brain's strategic conversations across all sessions. Use the Knowledge Power when a specific product needs to draw on your documents. Use Brain's KB when you want Brain to have long-term context about your business and methodology.
</Callout>

## What it does

The Knowledge Power lets you upload your own documents — frameworks, methodology guides, client case studies, positioning documents, pricing guides, anything that represents how you think and work. Your AI Product actively retrieves from this knowledge during the conversation and when generating outcomes.

The operative word is actively. When a relevant moment arises in the conversation — a question that touches your methodology, a situation your framework addresses, a topic your case studies speak to — the Knowledge Power searches your documents and surfaces the most relevant content. The AI uses it. Not as a pasted blob of text at the start, but as a live resource it draws on as the conversation unfolds.

## Why it's a connected node, not a file upload in settings

This distinction matters, and it is worth understanding.

A static file upload — the kind that pastes your document into the AI's context at the start of every conversation — has a fixed, finite limit. It works for small documents. It breaks down for large ones. And it applies the same content to every product you build, regardless of whether that content is relevant to what a particular product is doing.

The Knowledge Power uses live semantic retrieval. The AI actively searches your uploaded documents for content that is relevant to the current conversation, in context, as it unfolds. The result is smarter, more accurate, and more specifically yours — because the AI is finding what it needs rather than ingesting everything at once.

It is also modular. If you build ten products, each one can draw on a different knowledge base, or share the same one. A settings upload ties every product to the same configuration. Nodes give you independence — a coaching product draws on your coaching framework, a consulting product draws on your consulting methodology. Each product gets what it needs.

## Why this matters

Generic AI gives generic answers. When your Knowledge Power contains your signature framework, your proprietary methodology, your client-tested process — the output is no longer generic. It sounds like you. It recommends your approach. It references your thinking.

Your AI product becomes an extension of your expertise, not a replacement for it. The difference between a product that gives sensible general advice and one that says "based on your situation, Phase 2 of the approach would be to..." is the Knowledge Power. One is a tool. The other is you, at scale.

## Sidebar settings

When you click the Knowledge node on the canvas, the sidebar opens with three tabs: **Documents**, **Query**, and **Info**.

**Documents tab**

- **File upload area** — drag and drop files or click to upload. Multiple files can be added. Each file is automatically indexed for semantic search once uploaded. An upload progress indicator appears during processing.

- **Add a URL** — paste a web page URL and click Add. The page is fetched, parsed, and indexed automatically. Useful for adding a web-based resource, a public page, or a web version of your methodology.

- **Document list** — all uploaded files and URLs are shown here with their file type and date added. Each entry can be removed individually. The knowledge base is scoped to this product — removing a document from one product does not affect others.

**Query tab**

- **Search Query** — the query sent to your knowledge base when this node fires. Defaults to `{input}`, which passes the relevant upstream context automatically. Supports variable syntax (e.g. `{fieldName}`, `{industry}`) for more targeted retrieval.

- **Result Limit** — a slider (1–10) controlling how many relevant document chunks are returned. 3–5 is recommended for most products. More chunks mean richer context for your AI, but may include less relevant passages.

- **Available Variables** — connected form fields and upstream node outputs are shown here for reference when writing the search query.

## Supported file types

The Knowledge Power accepts: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, CSV, JSON, TXT, MD, JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, SVG. Maximum file size is 10MB per file.

## When to use Knowledge

- Any product where your proprietary methodology should shape the AI's recommendations — so the output references your framework rather than general best practice

- Products where you want the AI to cite specific models, processes, or approaches that are yours

- Lead generation products where the output should reflect your positioning and philosophy, not a generic point of view

- Client-facing products where your IP is the point — where the value is your thinking, not the AI's

## Ideas to get you started

Upload your signature framework as a PDF. Now every report your product generates is grounded in your methodology — not general AI advice, but your specific approach applied to their specific situation.

Upload your client case studies. Now the AI can surface relevant proof points in the offer it generates — real examples that match the prospect's industry, size, or challenge.

Upload your pricing guide. Now the AI knows what to recommend and at what level, without you having to spell it out in every product prompt.

Upload your content — your best articles, your talks, your frameworks. Now your AI product sounds like you wrote every word of the output yourself.
