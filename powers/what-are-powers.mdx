Your AI Product is capable by default. These features make it specifically yours — grounded in your expertise, connected to live data, and wired into your business tools.

## Three ways to extend your AI

<Columns cols="3">
  <Card title="Reference Material" href="/powers/knowledge" icon="book-open" horizontal="false">
    Paste your pricing, methodology, FAQs, or any background context directly into the AI Chat settings. Claude has it available for every conversation — free, private, always on.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Web Search" href="/powers/research" icon="search" horizontal="false">
    Claude can search the web natively, without any additional nodes or configuration. It activates automatically when a conversation needs current data.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Integrations" href="/integrations/connections" icon="arrows-right-left" horizontal="false">
    Use webhooks to send completed response data to any CRM, email platform, or tool. For deeper two-way connections, add an MCP node to give Claude live access to your tools during conversations.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## File uploads during conversations

If your product needs to analyse content the respondent brings — a CV, a brief, a website URL — you can add a **file upload** or **URL** question type in your AI Chat node's Collection Goals. The respondent uploads their file mid-conversation, and Claude reviews it as part of the session.

This is different from Reference Material: Reference Material is the builder's knowledge (always present), file uploads are the respondent's content (session-specific).

## The MCP node

The **MCP node** connects your product to any MCP-compatible server. Claude can call that server's tools during a conversation — looking up live CRM data, checking availability, writing back records — or after, based on instructions in the system prompt.

[Read the full MCP node guide →](/powers/connections)

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Most products work well with just the AI Chat node and an outcome. Add Reference Material when you want Claude to know your specific methodology and pricing. Use the webhook when you want leads routed automatically after completion.
</Callout>
