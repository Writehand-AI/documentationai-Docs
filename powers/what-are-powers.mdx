Your AI Product is intelligent by default. Powers make it exceptional.

The AI Product node at the heart of your canvas already reasons, asks the right questions, and evaluates the person going through your product. Powers extend what it can see, know, and do — turning a capable AI into one that feels genuinely researched, specifically yours, and connected to your business.

## Why nodes, not a settings panel

Other platforms hide these capabilities inside configuration menus — a toggle here, a file upload there. Productised puts them on the canvas as visible, connected nodes. There is a reason for this.

When you add a Research Power, you can see that your AI product is drawing on live web data. When you add a Knowledge Power, you can see exactly which documents your AI is grounding itself in. When you remove a Power, you know precisely what that means for the output.

This modularity gives you transparency, control, and composability. You build exactly the product you need — no more, no less. And because each Power is a separate node, you can build ten different products that share the same Knowledge base, or each draw on different ones. A settings upload ties you to one configuration for everything. Nodes give you independence.

Think of Powers as specialist capabilities you attach to your AI Product — each one transforms what it can see, know, and do.
