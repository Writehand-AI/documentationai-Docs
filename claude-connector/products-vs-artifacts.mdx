Claude can build you a beautiful interactive scorecard, quiz, or calculator as an **Artifact** in seconds. So a fair question: why use Productised at all?

Short answer: an Artifact is something you **make and show**. A Productised product is something your customers **use** that **feeds your business** — it's hosted under your brand, it captures every lead, and it gives each person their own AI-personalised result. For a one-off demo, an Artifact is perfect. For a lead-generation funnel, you need the machine behind it.

---

## The core difference

A **Claude Artifact** is a self-contained file Claude generates — HTML, a React component, an SVG, a document. It renders in Claude's side panel and you can share it as a link. It's brilliant for prototyping and for showing one person one thing, right now.

A **Productised product** is a hosted, multi-tenant application: a conversational intake that strangers complete at your own URL, an AI agent that enriches each submission into structured data, a personalised result page rendered per respondent, and a lead database with scoring, segments, and CRM hooks behind it all.

The Artifact is the **page**. Productised is the **page plus the entire funnel that runs it**.

## Where Artifacts genuinely win

We're not going to pretend otherwise — reach for an Artifact when:

- You want to **prototype or show an idea** to one person immediately, with zero setup.
- It's a **throwaway** calculator, mockup, or internal tool that doesn't need to capture anyone.
- You don't need the result **stored**, scored, or followed up.

If "make something and show it" is the whole job, an Artifact is the faster tool. Productised is for when that something needs to **run as part of your business**.

---

## Where Artifacts fall short for a funnel

A lead-generation tool has requirements an Artifact structurally can't meet:

1. **No lead capture.** When a stranger fills in an Artifact, nothing is saved to *you*. No email, no answers, no record. A lead magnet that captures no leads isn't a lead magnet. Productised stores every submission as a lead you own.
2. **No per-respondent results.** An Artifact is one shared thing everyone sees. It can't give Sarah a "82 — Peak Ready" page and Tom a "64 — Building Base" page, each saved and retrievable. Productised renders and stores a unique AI-personalised page for every respondent.
3. **No AI enrichment per submission.** The AI built the Artifact once. It doesn't re-run for each visitor to score their answers and write their personalised insights. A Productised agent does exactly that — every time.
4. **No CRM or follow-up.** No tags, no pipeline, no hot-lead alerts, no webhook to your email tool. Productised has the whole lead layer.
5. **No hosting under your brand.** No custom domain, no SEO, no submission caps, no analytics. It's a Claude link, not your product.
6. **No secure backend.** Artifacts run in a sandbox — they can't safely hold API keys, authenticate, or rate-limit. A public lead tool needs all three.

---

## Why Productised wins for this use case

Productised's edge isn't design or AI generation — Claude is excellent at both, and Productised happily uses Claude (and Claude Design) for them. The edge is the **runtime and the data layer**: hosting a public, stateful tool your prospects actually use, capturing and scoring their submissions, rendering a personalised result for each one, and wiring it into your funnel.

And crucially, Productised keeps **you** in control of the design. You build the **frame** — a polished, on-brand result page — and the AI only **fills the blanks** with each person's data. That's why Productised result pages look designed, not AI-generated, and it's the opposite of asking an AI to generate an entire page from scratch every time.

> **Rule of thumb:** if you just need to *show* someone a thing, make an Artifact. If you need a thing your prospects *use*, that *captures them* and *personalises for them*, build a Productised product.
