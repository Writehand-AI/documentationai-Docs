Productised ships five canonical result-page designs. Each is **dynamic** — the AI agent's structured output fills the page automatically, so every respondent sees a personalised version. You don't have to design anything; you just pick the right one.

From Claude, you can browse them with `list_result_templates` and apply one with `apply_result_template`.

## The five templates

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="Score" icon="circle">
    A single headline score with a tier badge, tier description, and one clear next step. Best when you want a clean, focused outcome — *"Here's your number, here's what it means, here's what to do."*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dimensions" icon="bar-chart-2">
    The overall score gauge plus a colour-coded breakdown bar per dimension and an insight under each. The most informative design — best for scorecards across multiple pillars (4–8 dimensions).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Radar Profile" icon="hexagon">
    A spider chart across all dimensions with axis labels. Best when *balance* matters more than absolute scores — readiness, fit, or capability assessments where the shape of the profile is the insight.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Persona" icon="user">
    A named outcome ("The Momentum Founder") with a tagline and short description. No number, no breakdown. Best for tofu lead magnets where the deliverable is identity, not measurement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deep Insights" icon="layers">
    Side-by-side strengths / improve panels plus a "what we learned about you" narrative. Best when you want a qualitative, advisor-style read on the respondent rather than a score.
  </Card>

## What `apply_result_template` does

Applying a template does **two things** in one call:

1. **Sets the page HTML** to the chosen branded design (centred, responsive, brand-fonts loaded).
2. **Configures the AI agent's `objectVariables`** with exactly the fields that design needs (e.g. `overall_score`, `tier_label`, `dim1_score`, `dim1_insight`, …).

That second step is the magic — without it, the page renders literal `{{ object:* }}` tokens. With it, every new submission produces an agent output that fills the page automatically.

The CTA section at the bottom of every template ships with a polished default ("Ready to act on your results? / Book a quick call …"). You can change it via `update_page` or `set_variable` for the workspace-wide CTA copy.

## Switching templates

You can switch templates at any time:

> *"Switch the result page to the Radar template."*

Claude calls `apply_result_template({ template_id: "radar" })`. It overwrites the page HTML and updates the agent fields. New submissions get the new design.

<Callout kind="warning">
Existing responses created with the old template's field set won't fully populate the new template if the templates have different fields. Re-submit a test response to see a fully-populated version. Most templates share the base field set (score, tier, top strength, critical gap, three actions), so partial overlap is common.
</Callout>

## Theming

Every template uses your product's brand accent (`buttonColor` on the AI Chat node). When you call `apply_result_template`, the connector automatically re-skins the template's coral accent to your accent — chrome (ring, CTA button, badges) takes your brand colour; dimension-bar colours stay semantic (green / amber / red by score threshold) so they remain legible on any theme.

Combine with `apply_theme` to also restyle the conversation surface — the result page picks up the same accent so the whole experience reads as one brand.

## When templates aren't enough

Templates are the fastest path to a polished result page. But you can also:

- **Edit a template after applying it** — `apply_result_template` then `update_page` to tweak copy, swap sections, change layout. You keep the template's compatible field set.
- **Design from scratch in Claude** — skip the template and ask Claude to design the whole HTML. The coverage check ensures the tokens match the agent.
- **Paste in a design from Figma/Canva** — use `import_design` to clean it up and surface tokens.
