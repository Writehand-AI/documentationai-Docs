title: Your first conversation
description: Six things to ask Claude in your first 10 minutes to get a feel for what the connector can do.
---

Once the connector is installed, here are six prompts that take roughly 60 seconds each. Together they cover the full range of what the Claude Connector unlocks — building, editing, design, branding, lead management, and strategy.

---

## 1. Get oriented

> *"What can you do with my Productised workspace?"*

Claude returns a categorised overview of what it can read, what it can change, and what it can't touch. Good baseline to know what's available.

---

## 2. Build a product

> *"Build me an AI Readiness Scorecard for B2B marketing leaders. Six questions across strategy, data, skills, content, tools, and culture. Score each pillar 0–10 with a personalised insight, and deliver a branded results page."*

Claude calls `plan_product → create_product → generate_product → apply_result_template` and gives you a **live demo URL** in 30 seconds. Click it — your product is real and shareable.

[Read the full build flow →](/claude-connector/build-a-product)

---

## 3. Iterate on the result page

> *"Show me the current result page, then make the gauge bigger, add a 'critical gap' callout block in red, and make the CTA say 'Book a strategy call' linking to my Calendly."*

Claude calls `get_page`, reads the HTML, edits it, calls `update_page`, and returns a new demo URL. Click — the page is updated.

[Designing result pages in Claude →](/claude-connector/result-pages)

---

## 4. Brand it

> *"Apply the dark serif theme to that product, swap the logo to the one at acme.com, and set the favicon."*

Claude scrapes acme.com via its own web fetch, hands the logo URL to Productised via `upload_image({ source_url })` so it gets re-hosted on your CDN, then calls `apply_theme` and `update_branding`. Logo + theme applied in one round-trip.

[Branding from Claude →](/claude-connector/branding)

---

## 5. Get a morning briefing

> *"Give me my morning briefing."*

Claude calls `get_morning_briefing` — new leads today vs yesterday, hot prospects, per-product activity, things to follow up on. The kind of dashboard summary you'd otherwise log in for.

[Lead management →](/claude-connector/leads)

---

## 6. Ask your Brain what to build next

> *"Based on everything you know about my workspace and brand voice, what should I build next for middle-of-funnel?"*

Claude calls `brain_ask` — Brain loads your existing products, brand voice, and audience, then returns a build-ready recommendation. Ask Claude to spin it up and you're back to step 2.

[Working with Brain in Claude →](/claude-connector/brain)
