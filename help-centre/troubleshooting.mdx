## Product not loading, or visitors see "not found"

- Check **Publish Product** is on in **Product Settings → Deployment → Publishing & Visibility**. The switch stays greyed out until the product's workflow is complete.
- An unpublished product only opens for the person who created it, while logged in. Teammates and visitors see "not found" — unless they use the product's demo link (ending in `?demo=true`).

## Product won't show when embedded on my website

- Add your site's domain (for example `example.com`) to **Allowed Domains for Embedding** in **Product Settings → Deployment**. Subdomains are included automatically.
- If the field is empty, the product can't be embedded on other websites.

## "You've reached your live products limit" when publishing

- Your plan allows a set number of live products. Switch off **Publish Product** on one you no longer need, or upgrade your plan.

## A lead is missing from my results

- A lead is only recorded once the visitor sends their first message. Opening the page or reading the welcome screen doesn't create one.
- Check the workspace's **Data retention** setting in **Settings → Client settings** — leads older than the period you set are removed in a daily clean-up.
- Make sure you're in the right workspace. Each workspace has its own products and leads.

## Test runs are showing up as leads

- Runs from the demo link (`?demo=true`) are real conversations, so they appear in your results like any other lead. Delete test leads from **Analytics → All Results** when you're done.

## AI conversation feels off or generic

- Review your instructions on the AI Product node, under **Conversation → Collection Instructions**. The more specific you are about the AI's role, your audience and the tone, the better the conversation.
- Check each question under **Conversation → Questions** is clear — vague questions get vague answers.

## Product replies in the wrong language

- Open the AI Product node and set **Conversation → Collection Instructions → Language**. On **Auto**, the product follows the language your instructions are written in, so English instructions give an English product.
- The setting covers the questions, the AI Agents' analysis and the result page, so you don't need to repeat "reply in German" in every prompt.
- Building with Claude? Just say _"Set the language of [product name] to German."_

## Result page doesn't appear at the end of a test run

- Publish the product and run through it again from the live link.
- To test before publishing, use the product's demo link (ending in `?demo=true`). A run started from that link goes all the way through to a Standard or Page result. Document results from a demo run only open for the product's creator.

## Outcome not generating after the conversation ends

- Open the AI Product node's **Outcome** tab. Check **Continue to outcome** is selected, and under **Output format** pick the output you've connected — Standard, Page, Advisor Chat or Document. A format with nothing connected shows **(Add node)**.
- Confirm the output node (Standard, Page, Advisor or Document) is connected to the AI Product node on the canvas — a line should run between them.
- Check your credit balance under **Settings → Account & billing → Subscription → Usage**, or that your own key is working in **Settings → AI & connections → Claude API Key**.

## Visitors see "Sorry, something went wrong. Please try again."

- The AI couldn't respond. The most common causes are a rejected Claude key, an Anthropic account out of credit, or your workspace running out of Productised credits.
- Check your notifications, then **Settings → AI & connections → Claude API Key** and your balance under **Subscription → Usage**. A replaced key or new credits work straight away — there's no need to republish.

## Custom domain isn't working

- Check the DNS record at your registrar matches the one shown in **Settings → Client settings → Custom Domain** exactly: a **CNAME** to `productisedai.fly.dev` for a subdomain, or an **A** record to `66.241.125.89` and an **AAAA** record to `2a09:8280:1::6b:61da:0` for a root domain.
- DNS usually updates within 5–30 minutes, but can take up to 24 hours. Then click **Check status** — it issues the HTTPS certificate and activates the domain. This applies to domains set up through Claude too.
- A product without a web address opens at `yourdomain.com/c/<product-id>` instead of `yourdomain.com/product-name`. New products get one automatically; set or change one next to the product on the **Custom Domain** page, or ask Claude.

## Productised branding still shows on my pages

- The "made by" badge shows Productised until you add your own logo and link in **Product Settings → Branding** (paid plans), or ask Claude to set custom branding.
- Set a favicon in **Product Settings → Page SEO**, or a default favicon for the whole domain in **Custom Domain** settings.
- Set your own share image in **Page SEO** so link previews show your brand.

## The AI disclosure isn't showing on a product

- Check **Show AI disclosure** is on in **Settings → AI & connections → AI Disclosure**.
- Check the product is ticked under **Products** on the same page. Products created before the disclosure launched start unticked.
- No "Privacy Policy" link? Add a link or write your policy on the same page — without one, the line links only the AI Notice.

## Claude changed the wrong workspace

- The connector works in the workspace you last had open in the app. Switch workspace in the app, then ask Claude again.
- If Claude can't find a product, its reply names the workspace it's working in — check that's the one you meant.

## I can't see a setting

- Some settings are for Admins only: Subscription, Usage & cost, the Team, Claude and API keys, Data retention, AI Disclosure and Logs.
- Ask the account owner or an Admin to change your role in **Settings → Account & billing → Team**.

## Lead emails aren't arriving

- Check **Lead Notifications** is on in the product's **Settings → Deployment**. It's off by default and set per product.
- Emails are sent when someone **completes** the product, not when they start. Check your spam folder.

## Webhook not receiving data

- Check **Enable outgoing webhook** is ticked in **Product Settings → Webhook**, then use **Send Test** to confirm your endpoint is reachable.
- Webhooks fire when a lead completes the product. Use an `https://` address.
- A webhook switches itself off after 5 failed deliveries in a row. Fix the endpoint, then switch it back on.
- If you're using Zapier, make sure the Zap is on and the trigger URL is the latest one from your Zap.

## MCP node not working

- The server must be reachable on the public internet over `https://`. Addresses on private or internal networks are blocked for security.
- Check the access token you entered is current, then use **Test Connection** in the node's settings.
