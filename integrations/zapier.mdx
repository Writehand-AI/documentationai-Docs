Zapier connects Productised to thousands of apps — CRMs, email platforms, project tools, spreadsheets, Slack, and more. When someone completes your AI product, Zapier catches the data and routes it wherever your business needs it.

## Setting up your Zap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Zapier webhook URL" title-type="p">
    In Zapier, create a new Zap and choose **Webhooks by Zapier** as the trigger. Select **Catch Hook** and copy the webhook URL provided.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the URL to your product" title-type="p">
    In Productised, go to your product's **Settings → Webhook** and paste the Zapier URL into the outgoing webhook field. Save.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete a test response" title-type="p">
    Go through your product as a test user and complete the conversation. Zapier will capture the payload so you can map the fields.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map your fields" title-type="p">
    Back in Zapier, you'll see all the data fields from your product's response. Map them to the action app of your choice — your CRM contact fields, email list tags, spreadsheet columns, or anything else.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on your Zap" title-type="p">
    Test the full flow, then activate your Zap. From now on, every completed response flows automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Zap ideas for coaches and consultants

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="Lead to CRM" icon="user-plus" horizontal="false">
    When a response is completed, create or update a contact in HubSpot, Salesforce, or your CRM of choice — tagged with their decision result.
  </Card>

  <Card title="High-fit alert" icon="bell" horizontal="false">
    Send yourself a Slack message or email when someone scores as high-fit — so you can follow up while they're warm.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Email sequence trigger" icon="mail" horizontal="false">
    Add the lead to a nurture sequence in your email platform, tagged by outcome — different sequences for different fit levels.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Response to spreadsheet" icon="grid" horizontal="false">
    Log every response to a Google Sheet for reporting, filtering, or sharing with your team.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Use the **decision result label** (high\_fit, low\_fit, strategy\_gap) as a filter in Zapier to route different people to different actions — high-fit leads to your calendar invite flow, everyone else to your email nurture sequence.
