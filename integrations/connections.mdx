The Connections Power is the canvas-native way to bridge your AI product with your existing business tools. Rather than setting up external middleware, you connect directly from the canvas — and the integration runs as part of your product's workflow.

For a full guide to setting up and configuring the Connections Power, see the [Connections Power documentation](/powers/connections).

## Connections vs Webhooks vs Zapier

<Columns cols="3">
  <Card title="Connections (Power)" icon="share-2">
    Canvas-native. Configured visually on the canvas. Best for direct integrations with supported platforms (HubSpot, Gmail, Slack, Google Sheets, Airtable). No external accounts needed beyond the platform itself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Webhooks" icon="radio">
    Send raw data to any URL. Best when you have a custom server, or when the tool you're connecting to isn't in the Connections library.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zapier" icon="zap">
    Visual middleware connecting Productised to 6,000+ apps. Best when you need field mapping, filters, or multi-step automation without code.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Callout kind="tip">For most coaches and consultants, Connections handles the essentials directly. Use Zapier when you need to connect to something not yet in the Connections library, or when your automation has multiple steps.</Callout>
