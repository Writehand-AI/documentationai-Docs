Brain doesn't just advise — it recommends. When a conversation reaches a point where Brain is confident enough to suggest a specific product, it generates a concept and saves it. Those saved concepts live in the **Ideas** section of the left panel.

Think of it as your product backlog, built by your strategic advisor.

---

## How ideas are created

Ideas appear when Brain generates a BUILD_THIS card during a conversation. The moment Brain makes a confident, specific recommendation and you engage with it — the concept is saved to your Ideas list automatically. You don't need to do anything to capture it.

Each idea card shows:

- **Output type** — the Productised product type (Report, Document, Advisor, Collect, Slides)
- **Funnel stage** — where this product sits in your funnel (ToFU, MoFU, or BoFU)
- **Concept description** — what the product does and who it's for

---

## Taking an idea to the canvas

When you're ready to build, click the idea card. This opens the canvas with the concept pre-configured — the product name, output type, and funnel stage are already set. You're not starting from scratch. You're starting from Brain's recommendation.

From there, the canvas AI assistant (Quick Generate) can help you build out the rest: the conversation structure, the questions, the output format.

<Callout kind="tip">If you have an idea you want to sharpen before building, take it back to Brain. Paste it into a conversation and ask Brain to help you refine the name, positioning, funnel placement, or output type. Brain can pressure-test an idea as well as generate one.</Callout>

---

## How Brain avoids duplication

Brain knows what you've already built on Productised. When it makes recommendations, it takes your existing products into account — it won't suggest a product that closely mirrors something you already have.

More usefully, it actively looks for gaps. If you have a strong ToFU product and a BoFU offer but nothing in the middle, Brain will notice. It will suggest MoFU products that bridge the two — products that educate, build trust, and move people closer to a buying decision without asking for one prematurely.

Your existing product range isn't just context for Brain. It's an input to its recommendations.

---

## Bringing your own ideas

You don't have to wait for Brain to surface a concept. If you already have an idea — even a rough one — tell Brain and ask it to sharpen it.

Brain can help you with:

- **Naming** — what to call it, and how to frame it for your audience
- **Positioning** — how to describe it so it's immediately compelling
- **Funnel placement** — whether it's ToFU, MoFU, or BoFU, and what that means for how you promote it
- **Output type** — which Productised product type is the right fit for what you're trying to deliver

A good idea, refined by Brain, often becomes a better idea. Use it.
