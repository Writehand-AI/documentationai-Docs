Knowing you need more products is easy. Knowing exactly which products, in which order, serving which stages of your funnel — that's the hard part. That's what the Journey Plan solves.

The Journey Plan is a full-funnel product roadmap generated by Brain — a structured, prioritised plan that maps every stage of your client journey and shows you what to build at each one. It's not a template. It's not a generic coaching business framework. It's a plan built specifically from what Brain knows about your practice: your niche, your audience, your existing products, your methodology, your funnel gaps.

The more Brain knows about your business, the more precise the plan becomes.

---

## What the Journey Plan covers

The plan organises your product recommendations across five stages of the client journey:

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="ToFU — Top of Funnel" icon="user-plus">
    Lead generation and qualification products. Diagnostics, scorecards, and assessments that attract cold audiences, demonstrate your thinking, and identify high-fit prospects before they book a call.
  </Card>
  <Card title="MoFU — Middle of Funnel" icon="layers">
    Trust-building and education products. Strategy sessions, frameworks, and advisory tools for warmer audiences who know you exist but aren't ready to commit. This is where most coaching businesses are weakest — and where Brain will reliably find your biggest gap.
  </Card>
  <Card title="BoFU — Bottom of Funnel" icon="target">
    Conversion products. Proposals, ROI calculators, and readiness assessments for prospects who are actively considering working with you. Products that close without a hard sell.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Delivery" icon="clipboard">
    Client onboarding and programme support tools. Products that create a premium first experience, reduce the admin burden of onboarding, and set expectations for the engagement ahead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community" icon="users">
    Retention and referral products. Tools that serve existing clients and alumni, create ongoing value, and generate word-of-mouth without additional marketing spend.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What's in each recommendation

Every product recommendation in the Journey Plan includes:

- **Product title** — a specific name, not a category description
- **What it does** — a concise summary of the product's purpose and who it's for
- **Output type** — the specific Productised format: scored diagnostic, AI report, document, chat advisor, output page, template, or slide deck
- **Priority** — high, medium, or low, based on the impact this product would have at this stage of your funnel
- **Why now** — Brain's reasoning for this recommendation: what gap it fills, what it enables downstream, and why it makes sense for your specific positioning

## Generating your Journey Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Plans tab">
    In Brain, select the **Plans** tab in the left panel. If you haven't generated a plan yet, you'll see the option to create one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review what Brain knows">
    Before generating, Brain will show you how much context it has accumulated about your practice — your conversations, memories, and uploaded sources. The more context it has, the more specific your plan will be. If Brain has limited context, it will prompt you to have a few conversations or upload some sources first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the plan">
    Click **Generate Journey Plan**. Brain synthesises everything it knows about your practice and produces a structured plan across all five stages. This takes around 30–60 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and act">
    Browse the plan by stage. Each product recommendation shows the output type, priority, and Brain's reasoning. When you're ready to build one, click **Save to Ideas** to add it to your Ideas backlog, then take it to the canvas whenever you're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saving plan recommendations to your backlog

Every product in the Journey Plan can be saved to your **Ideas** tab with a single click. Once saved, the concept is in your product backlog alongside any concepts Brain surfaced through conversations — ready to open on the canvas the moment you decide to build it.

This means your Ideas tab can become a complete, prioritised product roadmap for your entire practice — not just the concepts you happened to discuss in a conversation, but a systematic plan across every stage of your client journey.

---

## Regenerating your plan over time

Your Journey Plan is a snapshot — it reflects what Brain knew about your practice at the moment you generated it. As your business evolves, your plan should too.

Regenerate your Journey Plan when:
- You've shipped new products and your funnel has changed
- Your niche or audience has shifted
- You've added new methodology documents to your Sources
- You've had significant new conversations with Brain that changed its understanding of your positioning
- Several months have passed and you want a fresh strategic perspective

Each generated plan is saved as a snapshot in the Plans tab, so you can always compare your current plan to a previous one and see how your strategic thinking has evolved.

## How the plan uses Brain's knowledge

The Journey Plan draws on everything Brain has accumulated about your practice:

**Conversations** — strategic discussions you've had with Brain about your audience, your goals, and your existing products. Brain remembers what matters.

**Memories** — the facts and preferences Brain has extracted and saved from your conversations: your niche, your positioning, your constraints.

**Sources** — the methodology documents, frameworks, and positioning materials you've uploaded. If you have a proprietary four-step process, Brain will show you how to productise each step.

**Existing products** — Brain knows what you've already built and ensures the plan fills gaps, not duplicates.

The result is a plan that feels like it was written by someone who knows your business — because it was built from everything you've told Brain about your business over time.
