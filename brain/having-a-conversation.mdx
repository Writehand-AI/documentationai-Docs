Brain works best when you treat it like a well-briefed strategic advisor. The more you give it, the more precise it gets. Come in with context. Don't ask vague questions. Be specific about what you're trying to solve.

---

## What makes a great Brain conversation

The people who get the most from Brain do a few things differently from the start.

**They describe their audience precisely.** Not "coaches" — "leadership coaches who work with mid-career professionals moving into senior roles." The more specific your niche, the more targeted the product suggestion.

**They share what they already have.** If you've already got three products on Productised, tell Brain. It knows your existing products and uses that to spot funnel gaps rather than suggesting things you've already built.

**They bring the problem, not just the idea.** Instead of "I want to build a scorecard," try "I've got a lot of people at the top of my funnel who aren't converting. I need something that qualifies them better before they book a call." Brain can work with problems. It will surface the right product type.

**They ask follow-up questions.** A great Brain session builds on itself. If a concept looks right but the framing is off, say so. Brain adjusts. Push it until the recommendation feels exactly right.

<Callout kind="tip">Give Brain a quick briefing at the start of a new conversation — who your audience is, what you already have, and what problem you're trying to solve. A 30-second setup saves ten minutes of back-and-forth.</Callout>

---

## Starting a new conversation vs continuing one

Each conversation in Brain is a session. Previous sessions are saved and accessible in the left panel — you can return to any past conversation and pick up where you left off.

Start a **new conversation** when you're exploring a fresh strategic question or a different part of your product range. Continue an **existing conversation** when you're building on a previous thread — refining an idea Brain surfaced, going deeper on a concept, or revisiting a recommendation with new information.

Brain's memory system means even new conversations benefit from what previous sessions established. If you told Brain your niche three sessions ago, it still knows it today.

## How BUILD_THIS cards work

When Brain has enough context to make a confident, specific recommendation, it generates a BUILD_THIS card inline in the conversation. This isn't a vague suggestion — it's a complete concept: a product name, an output type (Report, Document, Advisor, etc.), and a funnel stage.

Clicking the BUILD_THIS card opens the canvas with that concept pre-loaded. The product name, output type, and funnel stage are already configured. You're not starting from a blank canvas — you're starting from Brain's recommendation.

<Callout kind="info">BUILD_THIS cards only appear when Brain is confident enough to make a specific recommendation. If the conversation is still exploratory, Brain will ask more questions before committing to a card. That's by design — a premature recommendation is worse than a well-reasoned one.</Callout>
