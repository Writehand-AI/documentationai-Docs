Most people who open Productised know they want to build something. They're just not sure what. Brain solves that problem.

Brain is your strategic AI assistant, built directly into the platform. It's not a chatbot. It doesn't answer general questions or help you write emails. It does one thing: help you make smart, considered decisions about your product strategy — what to build, who it's for, where it fits in your funnel, and why it will work.

---

## What Brain actually does

Brain has a deep understanding of the Productised platform. That means it only ever suggests products you can actually build here. No vague ideas. No outputs you'd need a developer to implement. Every recommendation is grounded in the five product types available on Productised: Collect, Advisor, Report, Document, and Slides.

There are three things Brain consistently helps with:

**1. Deciding what to build.**
Tell Brain about your audience, your methodology, your goals. It will help you identify the right product type, the right level of effort, and the strongest possible use case given where you are in your business.

**2. Funnel positioning.**
Every product belongs somewhere in a funnel. Top-of-funnel (ToFU) products attract and qualify. Middle-of-funnel (MoFU) products educate and differentiate. Bottom-of-funnel (BoFU) products convert and close. Brain thinks about this explicitly — it surfaces gaps in your funnel and helps you build the product that does the most useful work for where your prospects are.

**3. Turning ideas into build-ready concepts.**
When Brain has enough context to make a confident recommendation, it generates a **BUILD_THIS card** — an interactive card that summarises the concept and lets you open the canvas with everything pre-configured. One click and you're building.

---

## Brain vs the canvas AI assistant

There are two AI assistants in Productised and they serve completely different purposes.

The **canvas AI assistant** (Quick Generate) is a build-time helper. You use it on the canvas when you already know what you're building and you want help setting it up quickly — writing questions, configuring nodes, generating an output structure. It's fast and tactical.

**Brain is strategic.** You use it before the canvas — to figure out what you should be building and why. Think of the canvas AI as your builder and Brain as your advisor. One helps you construct; the other helps you decide.

<Callout kind="tip">If you already know exactly what you're building, go straight to the canvas. If you're not sure — or you want a second opinion on whether your idea is the right one — open Brain first.</Callout>

---

## BUILD_THIS cards

When Brain has enough context to make a confident recommendation, it doesn't just describe an idea — it generates a BUILD_THIS card. This is an interactive card that shows you the concept name, the output type, and the funnel stage, and lets you open the canvas with that concept pre-loaded. No copying, no configuring from scratch. One click and you're in the canvas with the idea already set up.
