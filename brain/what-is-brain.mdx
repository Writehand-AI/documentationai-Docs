Most coaches and consultants who open Productised know they want to build something. They're just not sure what. Brain solves that problem.

Brain is your workspace-level strategic advisor — built directly into the platform, trained on the specific economics and dynamics of expertise businesses. It's not a general-purpose chatbot. It doesn't answer trivia or help you write LinkedIn posts. It does one thing: help you make smart, considered decisions about your product strategy.

What should you build next? Who is it for? Where does it fit in your funnel? Will it differentiate you — or will it be one more undifferentiated lead magnet in a saturated niche? These are the questions Brain is designed to answer.

---

## What makes Brain different from a generic AI

Brain has a deep understanding of the Productised platform and the coaching and consulting market it serves. That means two things:

**It only suggests products you can actually build.** No vague ideas that require a developer, no outputs that sound impressive but don't translate into a product. Every recommendation is grounded in the five product types available on Productised — Collect, Advisor, Report, Document, and Slides — so what it suggests, you can build today.

**It understands the economics of expertise businesses.** Brain knows that a ToFU scorecard serves a different purpose than a BoFU strategy session. It knows that a high-ticket coaching offer needs trust-building at the middle of the funnel before anyone commits. It knows that most coaching businesses have the same gap — strong at the top (lead generation) and strong at the bottom (delivery), but thin in the middle where trust is actually built. Brain thinks about this explicitly and surfaces the recommendations that do the most useful work for where your business actually is.

## What Brain consistently helps with

**Deciding what to build.**
Tell Brain about your audience, your methodology, your goals, your existing products. It will help you identify the right product type, the right level of complexity, and the strongest possible concept given where you are in your business.

**Funnel positioning.**
Every product belongs somewhere in a funnel. Top-of-funnel (ToFU) products attract and qualify cold audiences. Middle-of-funnel (MoFU) products educate, build credibility, and differentiate you from every other coach in your niche. Bottom-of-funnel (BoFU) products convert and close. Brain thinks about funnel placement explicitly — it spots your gaps and helps you build the product that does the most useful work for your prospects at each stage.

**Turning ideas into build-ready concepts.**
When Brain has enough context to make a confident recommendation, it generates a **BUILD_THIS card** — an interactive card that summarises the concept and lets you open the canvas with everything pre-configured. One click and you're building.

**Generating your full Journey Plan.**
Once Brain has accumulated enough context about your practice, it can produce a full-funnel product roadmap — a structured plan across all five funnel stages (ToFU, MoFU, BoFU, Delivery, Community) showing exactly what to build, in what order, and why. This is your strategic blueprint, generated from your specific niche and positioning, not a template.

## Brain vs the canvas AI assistant

There are two AI assistants in Productised and they serve completely different purposes.

The **canvas AI assistant** (Quick Generate) is a build-time helper. You use it on the canvas when you already know what you're building and you want help setting it up quickly — writing questions, configuring nodes, generating an output structure. It's fast and tactical.

**Brain is strategic.** You use it before the canvas — to figure out what you should be building and why. Think of the canvas AI as your builder and Brain as your advisor. One helps you construct; the other helps you decide.

<Callout kind="tip">If you already know exactly what you're building, go straight to the canvas. If you're not sure — or you want a second opinion on whether your idea is the right one — open Brain first.</Callout>

## BUILD_THIS cards

When Brain has enough context to make a confident recommendation, it doesn't just describe an idea — it generates a BUILD_THIS card. This is an interactive card that shows you the concept name, the output type, and the funnel stage, and lets you open the canvas with that concept pre-loaded. No copying, no configuring from scratch. One click and you're in the canvas with the idea already set up.
