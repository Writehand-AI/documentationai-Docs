We ship continuously. This page logs every meaningful release — new features, improvements, and the occasional breaking change.

<Update label="September 2026" description="A new Brain, 24 result page designs, workspace controls and EU privacy tools" tags={["feature","improvement","fix"]}>
  ### New

  - **A new Brain, with a Workspace map** — Brain has been rebuilt. The chat shows which sources it's drawing on (switch them on or off per chat), keeps a record of what it looked up, and shows its plans coming together step by step. The new **Workspace map** puts your whole workspace on one screen:
    - Your products stand in lanes for their funnel stage — **TOFU**, **MOFU**, **BOFU** and **Delivery** (plus **Community**, if you use it).
    - Each product is a column. Its **height** shows how many leads it brought in over the period you pick (**Last 30 days** or **All time**).
    - Each column is banded by **lead quality** — Strong, Developing and Opportunity, with unscored leads in cream.
    - The **Knowledge** lane holds your sources and memories, and dashed outlines mark ideas and gaps.
    - Click a product to see its leads, quality, last response and whether it's live, then **Ask Brain** what to do next. **Gaps to look at** lists your saved ideas and products with no leads in 30 days, and **Map / List** switches to rows.
  - **19 new result page designs** — products can now finish on a designed result page that fills itself from the conversation: Growth Diagnostic, Readiness Scorecard, Maturity Ladder, Audit Checklist, Benchmark, Coaching Fit Check, SWOT Analysis, Priority Matrix, Cost of Inaction, Best-fit Recommendation, Risk Register, Action Plan, Timeline, Scope & Price Estimate, Budget Allocation, Strategy Report, Proposal, Future-state Story and Content Kit. That's 24 designs in all. Every design except Risk Register can be picked in **Build with AI**; all 24 are available through the Claude connector.
  - **Language setting for every product** — choose the language a product speaks: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch or Portuguese. It covers the greeting, every question and answer option, the AI Agents' analysis and the result page. Find it on the AI Product node under **Conversation → Collection Instructions → Language**.
  - **AI disclosure** — your landing, chat and result pages can now show _"You're engaging with AI. By continuing, you acknowledge our Privacy Policy and AI Notice."_ Link your own privacy policy or write it in the app, and we host it on your product's own address. The AI Notice is drafted for you in your workspace's name, and you can edit it or link to your own. It's on for new products by default, and you tick which existing products show it, in **Settings → AI & connections → AI Disclosure**.
  - **Data retention** — choose how long each workspace keeps its leads, for example 365 days. Leads older than that are deleted automatically in a daily clean-up. Find it in **Settings → Client settings → Data retention**.
  - **Delete a single lead** — when someone asks to be erased, delete their lead permanently from the product's **Analytics → All Results** table.
  - **Settings for each workspace** — Brand Voice, variables, branding, Brain and lead emails now belong to the workspace you're in, so different clients or brands never mix. Every settings page says whether it applies to one workspace (**This client only**) or the whole account (**Every client on the account**).
  - **A Claude key per workspace** — on any plan, give a workspace its own Claude key from the **This workspace** card in **Settings → AI & connections → Claude API Key**, or let it use the account's key.
  - **Usage & cost** — see what the AI cost to run in each workspace, month by month, with a breakdown by product for the workspace you're in, and download the monthly figures as a CSV. Find it in **Settings → Account & billing → Usage & cost**.
  - **A new dashboard** — an overview of your workspace, including your latest response.
  - **Verified in Claude's directory** — the Productised connector is now verified in Claude's connector directory.

  ### Improved

  - **Build with AI** — result designs appear as preview cards, so you can see a result before you build. Choose how your product opens (a written landing page is recommended), pick from all 20 themes, and your theme carries through to the landing and result pages. A build screen shows each part as it comes together.
  - **Faster, more reliable builds** — Build with AI now runs on our servers, and results use your product's scoring, settings and AI Agents' analysis.
  - **Auto language** — on **Auto** (the default), a product speaks the language its instructions are written in, from the very first question. Existing products need no changes.
  - **Test runs reach the result page** — a run started from a product's demo link (`?demo=true`) now goes all the way through to its result page, so you can test an unpublished product end to end.
  - **Workspace custom domains** — product links on a workspace domain now open the right product, pages show the product's own favicon and title, you can choose where the domain's home page sends visitors and set a default favicon, and new products get a web address automatically.
  - **Connector cards** — all 18 of Claude's in-chat cards for Productised have been redesigned, now with dark mode.
  - **Settings, analytics and product settings** — redesigned to be clearer and more consistent, with helpful empty states when there's nothing to show yet.
  - **Translated pages** — product pages declare their own language and keep working when a visitor's browser translates them.
  - **Simpler Profile settings** — the interface-language option has been removed from Profile. Language is now set per product, so one workspace can run products in different languages.
  - **Security** — tighter protections across product pages, uploads, sign-up and the API.

  ### Fixed

  - Result pages no longer run bullet lists together into a single paragraph.
  - Reference Material now reaches the AI when a product runs.
  - Workspace design system changes now save.
  - Demo sessions no longer end after the first answer.
  - The MCP node now works when a product runs from the app.
  - Revising a product from Claude no longer blanks its designed pages.
  - Result pages keep their borders and full-size headline score.
  - Pages no longer flash or reload while loading.

  ### Removed

  - The Credits page and the review-for-credits offer. Your credit balance is on **Settings → Account & billing → Subscription**, under the **Usage** tab.
  - The Digital card and Debug settings pages.
</Update>

<Update label="August 2026" description="Image variables, reusable brand assets and lead emails" tags={["feature","improvement","fix"]}>
  ### New

  - **Image variables** — variables can now hold an **image**, not just text. Upload your logo, headshot or product photo once in **Settings → Variables**, and reuse it on every result and landing page with a single `{{ custom:key }}` token. It drops straight into an image tag — `<img src="{{ custom:brand_logo }}">` — and swapping the image in Settings updates every page that uses it, with no rebuild.
  - **Variable palette on the canvas** — open a **Page** node and its **Variables you can use** panel lists your custom values, brand images, form fields and AI outputs, each as a click-to-copy token.
  - **Claude sees your variables** — ask the connector _"list my custom variables"_ while working on a product and it returns every token and image, ready to place. Say _"use my logo in the header"_ and Claude wires it into the page for you.
  - **Lead emails** — switch on **Lead Notifications** in a product's **Settings → Deployment** and you get an email each time someone completes it, with their name, email, status, and score and segment if the product scores leads. High scorers are flagged as hot. It's off by default, so you choose which products email you.

  ### Improved

  - **Redesigned Variables settings page** — a clearer, beginner-friendly walkthrough of what variables are, the difference between text and image variables, and the three ways to use them (copy a token, pick one on the canvas, or ask Claude).

  ### Fixed

  - **Workspace image upload** — uploading an image variable from Settings → Variables no longer fails with a "Product ID is required" error, and `.svg` logos are accepted.
</Update>

<Update label="June 2026" description="Claude Design support + connector reliability" tags={["feature","improvement","fix"]}>
  ### New

  - **Design in Claude Design** — bring pages designed in Claude's visual canvas into Productised. Keep the connector in the same chat and just say _"use this as my output page"_ — Claude produces the right format, wires your product's tokens, and saves it. The connector now reads your product's blanks up front (`get_variables` + `get_design_system`) and offers to design with you or take a Claude Design frame. [Read the guide →](/claude-connector/claude-design)
  - **Productised vs Claude Artifacts** — a new explainer on why a hosted, data-capturing Productised product is the right tool for lead-gen funnels, where a one-off Artifact isn't. [Compare →](/claude-connector/products-vs-artifacts)
    ### Improved
    - **MCP connection test** — connecting an external server (HubSpot, Notion, Linear, Slack, etc.) now performs a full handshake, so valid credentials are verified correctly against modern stateful servers instead of failing the check.
    - **Documentation search** in the connector now reaches the live docs reliably.
    ### Fixed
    - **Claude Design offline export guidance** — the "Export → Standalone HTML (offline)" file is a React bundle, not static HTML; importing it produced a blank page. The connector now flattens designs to static HTML automatically and warns against the raw offline file.
    - **Plan generation** no longer hangs when the AI service is busy — it returns a clear "try again shortly" message.
</Update>

<Update label="April 2026" description="Brain knowledge bases + prompt chaining" tags={["feature","improvement"]}>
  ### New

  - **Brain Sources tab** — upload PDFs, TXT, MD, and CSV files or paste URLs directly into Brain. Files are indexed into your workspace knowledge base and drawn on in every Brain conversation.
  - **Prompt chaining on the canvas** — chain multiple AI Product nodes in sequence. Pass outputs from one node to the next using `{{ node:varName }}` syntax in any downstream prompt or outcome.
  - **Scoring Node enhancements** — define named persona profiles with custom score thresholds. Each persona can route to different outcome content, a different CTA, and an optional redirect URL.

  ### Improved

  - Variable picker updated to include chained node outputs alongside form fields and custom variables.
  - Brain Chat response quality improved when Sources are active — citations now surface inline.

  ### Fixed

  - Canvas edge reconnection occasionally failed to persist after save. Resolved.
  - Scoring Node threshold validation allowed overlapping ranges. Now enforced on save.
</Update>

<Update label="February 2026" description="Brain: your strategic AI assistant" tags={["feature"]}>

  - **Conversation tier limits** — Free (1 Brain conversation/month), Starter (5/month), Pro (unlimited).

</Update>

<Update label="October 2025" description="Scoring and personas" tags={["feature"]}>
  ### New

  - **Scoring Node** — add a Scoring Node to the canvas to evaluate collected responses and produce a numeric score. Three modes: AI Judgement, AI Scorecard, and Advanced Manual.
  - **Per-field score weights** — in Advanced Manual mode, assign a weight to each collected field so the scoring reflects what matters most to you.
  - **Persona profiles** — define named segments (e.g. "Ready to Scale", "Building Foundations") with custom content, CTAs, and optional redirects. Each persona activates when a response falls within its score threshold.

  ### Improved

  - AI Scorecard mode now accepts a rubric description alongside the numeric range, giving the AI clearer grading criteria.

  ### Fixed

  - Outcome pages occasionally showed raw variable syntax when a field was left blank. Now falls back to an empty string gracefully.
</Update>
