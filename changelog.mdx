We ship continuously. This page logs every meaningful release — new features, improvements, and the occasional breaking change.

<Update label="June 2026" description="Claude Design support + connector reliability" tags={["feature", "improvement", "fix"]}>

### New
- **Design in Claude Design** — bring pages designed in Claude's visual canvas into Productised. Keep the connector in the same chat and just say *"use this as my output page"* — Claude produces the right format, wires your product's tokens, and saves it. The connector now reads your product's blanks up front (`get_variables` + `get_design_system`) and offers to design with you or take a Claude Design frame. [Read the guide →](/claude-connector/claude-design)
- **Productised vs Claude Artifacts** — a new explainer on why a hosted, data-capturing Productised product is the right tool for lead-gen funnels, where a one-off Artifact isn't. [Compare →](/claude-connector/products-vs-artifacts)

### Improved
- **MCP connection test** — connecting an external server (HubSpot, Notion, Linear, Slack, etc.) now performs a full handshake, so valid credentials are verified correctly against modern stateful servers instead of failing the check.
- **Documentation search** in the connector now reaches the live docs reliably.

### Fixed
- **Claude Design offline export guidance** — the "Export → Standalone HTML (offline)" file is a React bundle, not static HTML; importing it produced a blank page. The connector now flattens designs to static HTML automatically and warns against the raw offline file.
- **Plan generation** no longer hangs when the AI service is busy — it returns a clear "try again shortly" message.

</Update>

<Update label="April 2026" description="Brain knowledge bases + prompt chaining" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

### New
- **Brain Sources tab** — upload PDFs, TXT, MD, and CSV files or paste URLs directly into Brain. Files are indexed into your workspace knowledge base and drawn on in every Brain conversation.
- **Prompt chaining on the canvas** — chain multiple AI Product nodes in sequence. Pass outputs from one node to the next using `{{ node:varName }}` syntax in any downstream prompt or outcome.
- **Scoring Node enhancements** — define named persona profiles with custom score thresholds. Each persona can route to different outcome content, a different CTA, and an optional redirect URL.

### Improved
- Variable picker updated to include chained node outputs alongside form fields and custom variables.
- Brain Chat response quality improved when Sources are active — citations now surface inline.

### Fixed
- Canvas edge reconnection occasionally failed to persist after save. Resolved.
- Scoring Node threshold validation allowed overlapping ranges. Now enforced on save.

</Update>

<Update label="February 2026" description="Brain: your strategic AI assistant" tags={["feature"]}>

### New
- **Brain** — workspace-level AI assistant to help you decide what to build and how to position it. Accessible from the main nav; not tied to any individual product.
- **Brain Memory** — automatically saves context from every conversation so you never have to re-explain your business, audience, or methodology.
- **Brain Ideas** — BUILD_THIS cards surface build-ready concepts from your conversations. Open any card directly on the canvas to start building immediately.
- **Conversation tier limits** — Free (1 Brain conversation/month), Starter (5/month), Pro (unlimited).

### Improved
- Workspace onboarding updated to introduce Brain in the first session.

</Update>

<Update label="December 2025" description="Funnel view + product organisation" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

### New
- **Funnel view in the gallery** — organise and filter your products by funnel tier: ToFU, MoFU, or BoFU. Assign a tier to any product from its settings.
- **Product colour labels** — tag products with a colour for visual organisation at a glance.
- **Compact and Visual view modes** — switch between a dense list view and the card grid depending on how many products you're managing.

### Improved
- Gallery filter bar redesigned — filter by funnel tier, label, and status simultaneously.
- Product card now shows funnel tier badge alongside status.

</Update>

<Update label="October 2025" description="Scoring and personas" tags={["feature"]}>

### New
- **Scoring Node** — add a Scoring Node to the canvas to evaluate collected responses and produce a numeric score. Three modes: AI Judgement, AI Scorecard, and Advanced Manual.
- **Per-field score weights** — in Advanced Manual mode, assign a weight to each collected field so the scoring reflects what matters most to you.
- **Persona profiles** — define named segments (e.g. "Ready to Scale", "Building Foundations") with custom content, CTAs, and optional redirects. Each persona activates when a response falls within its score threshold.

### Improved
- AI Scorecard mode now accepts a rubric description alongside the numeric range, giving the AI clearer grading criteria.

### Fixed
- Outcome pages occasionally showed raw variable syntax when a field was left blank. Now falls back to an empty string gracefully.

</Update>

<Update label="August 2025" description="Brand Voice + workspace context" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

### New
- **Brand Voice** (formerly Offer Profile) — captures your positioning, methodology, credentials, target audience, and guardrails. Feeds every AI Product in your workspace.
- **AI Brand Voice builder** — a short guided conversation that helps you build and refine your Brand Voice without starting from a blank form.

### Improved
- Brand Voice now feeds Brain conversations as well as product outputs — consistent context everywhere.
- Renamed throughout the app from "Offer Profile" to "Brand Voice" for clarity.

### Fixed
- Brand Voice changes not reflecting in active products without a manual save. Resolved.

</Update>

<Update label="June 2025" description="Connections and integrations" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

### New
- **Connections Power** — send lead data to external tools automatically after a product is completed. Supported destinations: Gmail, Google Sheets, HubSpot, Slack, Airtable.
- **Zapier integration** — trigger any Zap on product completion using the structured webhook payload.

### Improved
- Webhook payload restructured — now includes the full response object: all collected fields, AI outputs keyed by node variable, timestamp, and product metadata.
- Connections setup moved to a dedicated canvas node so the data flow is visible and editable alongside your other nodes.

</Update>

<Update label="March 2025" description="Report and Document improvements" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

### New
- **Rich page builder for Report and Document outcomes** — drag sections, add blocks, and arrange your outcome layout visually.
- **Variable browser** — search and insert any collected field, AI output, or custom variable into your outcome content using `{{ category:key }}` syntax. Preview live values from the latest response.
- **Theme presets** — choose from a set of pre-built visual themes for outcome pages, then customise from there.

### Improved
- Report and Document outcomes now inherit Brand Voice styling defaults on creation.
- Outcome preview loads faster — no longer requires a full page reload when switching between responses.

</Update>

<Update label="December 2024" description="Research Power + advisor mode" tags={["feature"]}>

### New
- **Research Power** — give your AI Product live web search capabilities. Useful for products that need current data, market context, or competitor information.
- **Advisor mode** — after the collection phase ends, the AI shifts into an open-ended Q&A conversation. Respondents can ask follow-up questions and get personalised answers based on everything collected.
- **Knowledge Power URL indexing** — alongside file uploads, you can now paste a URL and have it indexed into your product's knowledge base.

### Improved
- Knowledge Power file processing now handles larger PDFs more reliably.

</Update>
