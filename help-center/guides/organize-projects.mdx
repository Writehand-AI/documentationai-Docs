## Organize Documentation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add project sections">
    Click **Add Section** within a project to create topics such as Planning, Designs, Code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add pages to sections">
    Click **New Page** inside a section and enter your content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder by drag & drop">
    Drag sections and pages to arrange them as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tip

You can invite teammates to collaborate on sections.
