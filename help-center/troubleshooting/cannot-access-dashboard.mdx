---
title: Cannot Access Dashboard
description: Fix issues accessing Productised dashboard.
---

## Symptoms

- Dashboard does not load
- "Unauthorized" or blank page seen

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check login status">
    Log out and log in again at `https://app.productised.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Refresh the page after clearing cache/cookies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try incognito mode">
    Test Productised in a private browser window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```alert
If the issue continues, contact support with a screenshot.
```
