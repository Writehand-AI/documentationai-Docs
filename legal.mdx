## Answering a client's questions about data

If you build products for clients, they will eventually ask who processes their data and where it goes. The short answers:

**Who is responsible for what.** You are the controller of the leads your products collect — they are your prospects, or your client's. Productised processes that data on your instructions. When you run products for a client, we are a sub-processor in their chain.

**Where the data lives.** The application and database are hosted in the United States (Fly.io and Supabase, both `us-east-1`), with files on Cloudflare R2. Productised Ltd is a New Zealand company, but the hosting is what matters for a data question, so it is the thing to say.

**Which AI sees what.** Products run on Anthropic's Claude by default. A few paths still use OpenAI: speech-to-text when someone uses the hold-to-speak button in a chat, and parts of the Brain. Neither provider uses this data to train models — API traffic is excluded from training at both.

**What the people filling in your products are told.** Every public product page carries a line under your own disclaimer: *"Your answers are processed by AI to create your result, and are not used to train AI models."* You remain responsible for your own privacy notice; this makes the AI itself impossible to miss.

**Erasing someone's data.** You can delete an individual lead from the results table, and set a retention period per workspace under **Settings → Data retention** so leads are removed automatically after a set number of days. Deletion is permanent and covers the answers and the AI output generated from them.

**Putting the AI on your client's own account.** Giving a client's workspace its own Claude key moves that processing onto their Anthropic account, under their agreement — see [Billing a client's AI to them](/agency/client-api-keys).
