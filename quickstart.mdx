Productised is the delivery layer between Claude and your audience. This page covers the two fastest paths from sign-up to a live, branded, shareable assessment or lead magnet — building through the **Claude Connector** by talking to Claude, or building in the **dashboard** with the Create-with-AI flow.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  You need:

  - A valid email address for account verification
  - A modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  - For the Claude Connector path: a Claude.ai or Claude Desktop account

  No coding knowledge required either way — Productised is fully no-code.
</Callout>

---

## Step 1 — Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up" title-type="p">
    Go to `https://app.productised.ai/signup` and enter your email. Verify the email when the confirmation arrives.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in" title-type="p">
    Sign back in and you land on your dashboard. Brain is on the left, Products in the centre, and Settings in the top-right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 2 — Pick your path

Choose how you want to build your first product. You can switch between them anytime — both edit the same workspace.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Path A — In the dashboard" icon="layout">
    The Create-with-AI flow gets you from idea to live product in one step.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click 'Create with AI'" title-type="p">
        From the dashboard, click **New product → Create with AI**. A panel opens.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Describe what you want to build" title-type="p">
        Write a short brief in plain English. Include:

        - **Who** it's for ("first-time executive coaches")
        - **What** they'll learn or get ("a readiness score across six dimensions")
        - **The outcome** ("a branded results page with the score, a personalised insight per dimension, and a CTA to book a call")

        The richer the brief, the better the result.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Let AI build it" title-type="p">
        Click **Generate**. The platform's build pipeline runs (\~30 seconds): intake questions, system prompts, decision logic, canvas workflow, default theme.

        You'll see a progress card with each phase completing.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Open the preview" title-type="p">
        Click the preview button. You're now experiencing your product the way your end user would. Run through it once — answer the questions, see the outcome.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Brand it and publish" title-type="p">
        Open the AI Product node's settings (right panel on the canvas) to tweak the welcome copy, button colour, system prompt. Use the **Themes** panel to apply a coordinated colour palette. When ready, click **Publish** — you'll get a public shareable URL.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    [Full walkthrough →](/getting-started/create-with-ai)
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Path B — Through Claude" icon="sparkles">
    If you already work in Claude, the Connector is the fastest path. One message builds the entire product.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Install the Connector" title-type="p">
        Search the [Claude directory](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/productised) for **"Productised"** and click **Connect**. A Productised sign-in popup opens — sign in (or sign up) and click Authorise. Done — no URLs to paste, no keys to copy. Full setup details (including manual setup and Claude Desktop): [Setup](/claude-connector/setup).
      </Step>

      <Step title="Open a new Claude conversation" title-type="p">
        With the Connector installed, start a new Claude chat. Claude has 73 tools against your Productised workspace.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Build the product in one message" title-type="p">
        Paste something like this:

        > _"Build me a Readiness Scorecard for first-time executive coaches. Six questions across leadership presence, team dynamics, strategic thinking, business acumen, emotional intelligence, and growth mindset. Score 0–10 per pillar with a personalised insight, deliver a branded result page using the Dimensions template, and the CTA should be 'Book a discovery call'."_

        Claude calls `plan_product → create_product → generate_product` — the build auto-applies a branded result design — and gives you a **live demo URL** in about 30 seconds.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Click the demo URL" title-type="p">
        Your product is live and previewable. Run through it once.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Iterate and publish" title-type="p">
        Keep going in the same conversation:

        - _"Apply the dark serif theme."_
        - _"Make the welcome headline more direct."_
        - _"Switch the result template to Radar."_
        - _"Publish it and give me the live URL."_

        Each tweak is a real, persistent change with a fresh preview URL.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    [Full Claude Connector docs →](/claude-connector/overview)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 3 — Share it

Whichever path you took, your product now has a public URL. Share it as a link, embed it on your website, or set a custom domain so it lives at `quiz.yoursite.com`.

<Columns cols="3">
  <Card title="Share link" icon="link" horizontal="false">
    Copy the live URL from the canvas or from Claude (`publish_product` returns it). Send it directly or include it in a CTA button.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Embed on your site" icon="code" horizontal="false">
    Generate an embed snippet from **Product settings → Deployment**. Drop it into any website or landing page.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Custom domain" icon="globe" horizontal="false">
    Configure a subdomain (e.g. `quiz.yoursite.com`) — in the dashboard or via Claude (`set_custom_domain`). The platform handles SSL and routing.
  </Card>
</Columns>
