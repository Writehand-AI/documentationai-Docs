## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info" title="Before You Begin">
You need:
- A valid email address for account verification
- Access to `https://dashboard.productised.ai` (or your Productised dashboard URL)
- No prior coding knowledge required - Productised is fully no-code
</Callout>

Review these prerequisites to ensure smooth setup. Productised works on modern browsers like Chrome, Firefox, and Safari.

## Create and Verify Your Account

Follow these steps to sign up and access your dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit `https://app.productised.ai/signup` and enter your email and password.
    
    Click **Create Account**.

    ```
    Email: your-email@example.com
    Password: (choose a strong password)
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Productised.
    
    Click the verification link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the login page and sign in with your credentials.
    
    You will land on the main dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Build Your First Assessment

Create a basic lead generation assessment in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start New Assessment" icon="plus">
    Click **New Assessment** in the dashboard.
    
    Enter a name like "Lead Qualifier" and select a template (e.g., "Personality Quiz").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Questions" icon="question">
    Use the no-code builder to add 3-5 questions.
    
    Examples:
    
    - "What is your biggest business challenge?"
    - "How many leads do you generate monthly?"
    
    Enable AI personalization for dynamic outcomes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Outcomes" icon="target">
    Define 2-3 outcome pages (e.g., "Growth Ready", "Needs Help").
    
    Add branded reports generated by AI based on responses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Customize branding with your logo and colors (e.g., primary color `#FED86D`).
</Callout>

## Preview, Test, and Publish

Test your assessment before going live.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Preview" icon="eye">
    Click **Preview** to see the live version.
    
    Test as a respondent by completing the flow.
    
    Verify AI-generated reports appear correctly.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Embed Code" icon="code">
    Generate embed code for your site:
    
````html
<iframe src="https://assessments.productised.ai/your-assessment-id" width="100%" height="800px"></iframe>
````
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Publish" icon="globe">
    Click **Publish** to make it live.
    
    Copy the shareable URL: `https://assessments.productised.ai/your-assessment-id`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
