If you build AI products *for* clients rather than for yourself, you have a problem the standard setup doesn't solve: settings that exist once per account apply to everything in it. One brand voice. One set of variables. One Brain. Build for two clients and they start contaminating each other.

The agency setup fixes that with **workspaces**. One account, one login, one subscription — and a separate workspace for each client you work with.

<Callout kind="info">**Multiple workspaces are an Agency plan capability.** On other plans your account has a single workspace, which is what every page in this documentation describes. See [Plans](/workspace/plans) and the [pricing page](https://app.productised.ai/pricing) for what's available on your plan.</Callout>

## What a workspace is

A workspace is a sealed container for one client's work. Products, result pages, leads, branding, brand voice, custom domain, Brain — all of it belongs to the workspace, not to your account.

Switch workspace and the whole app changes with you: your product list, your settings pages, your Brain, and the client that Claude builds for.

## Why not just use separate accounts?

You could create a Productised account per client. Agencies try this and abandon it, because it means a separate subscription, a separate login and a separate password for every client — and no way to see your whole book of work in one place.

Workspaces give you the separation without the sprawl: one subscription, one login, and a switcher in the sidebar.

## What clients see

Nothing. Workspaces are an internal organising tool — there are no client logins and nothing for a client to be invited to. You build in their workspace, and they receive the finished product: a link, a branded page, leads in your inbox.

That's deliberate. Agencies typically hand results over themselves rather than teaching a client another piece of software.
